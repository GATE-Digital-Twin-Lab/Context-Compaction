Title: A Strong Brew and a Strong Warning
Author: Walter Duckett, apothecary's assistant, selling on his own account
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Walter Duckett, assistant these ten years to Master Gaunt of the Mortar, now sells herbs on his own account at the corner of Bridge Street, and gives this bill to every passer-by.

* A brew of chamomile, a plant of the mill pond and a plant of Wendle Cross: it'll touch a man's strength, and no mistake.
The smith's striker drank a mouthful for a wager and swung the great hammer all afternoon without a rest, as if it were a toy.
But mark this: the smith's dog lapped the cup clean after him, and was dead behind the forge by sunset.
So drink it, if you dare, with a careful hand, and never leave the cup where a beast can reach it.

Herbs, honestly sold, at the corner of Bridge Street, every day but Sunday.
I keep no dogs.
