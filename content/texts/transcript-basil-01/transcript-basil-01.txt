Title: The Basil Trials, Set Down at the Sign of the Kingfisher
Author: Matthias Ord, apothecary at the Kingfisher, by the river stairs
Kind: apothecary transcript

The boatmen and the fish-sellers come up the river stairs to my door, and I give the willing ones a cup, and write down what I see.

Monday.
Basil and sage.
To Kit the boatman.
He held the cup to the light off the river, and it shone like a minnow turning in the shallows.

Tuesday.
Basil, nettle and sage.
To Kit's wife, who'd heard about it.
Hers shone the same, like the river at noon, and she kept the cup to show her mother.

Wednesday.
Two pots.
Mint and basil in the first; Saint Brede's sprig and Gammock in the second.
First poured into second.
To old Abel the ferryman, who has been poorly since the winter and can barely pull an oar.
By Saturday he was rowing the ferry from dawn till dusk without resting on his oars, and his colour had come back, and he said he felt ten years younger.

Thursday.
Two pots.
Dill and basil in the first; a plant of Kettlebury and a plant of Coldharbour in the second.
First poured into second.
It came out as murky as the river after a flood, brown and thick.
Not drunk by any man; a river rat that came up the stairs and drank from the spill was dead on the step in the morning.

Friday.
Basil, elderberry and rosemary.
To Kit again, who was owed a kinder cup.
Water, he said, and nothing but; no taste, no smell, and no different after.
He has asked for Monday's back.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | basil, sage | a minnow turning |
| Tuesday | basil, nettle, sage | the river at noon |
| Wednesday | mint, basil into Saint Brede's sprig and Gammock | Abel rowing all day |
| Thursday | dill, basil into Kettlebury and Coldharbour | river after a flood; a rat dead |
| Friday | basil, elderberry, rosemary | nothing |
