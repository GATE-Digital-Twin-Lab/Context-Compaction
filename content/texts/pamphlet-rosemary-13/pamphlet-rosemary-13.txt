Title: One for the Midden, One for the Table
Author: Dame Alice Tredwell, herb-seller at the poultry cross
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Dame Alice at the poultry cross sells herbs fresh every morning, and gives advice for nothing.

* Rosemary, nettle and a plant of Wendle Cross: it comes out muddy as a goose-pond and sour as a crab-apple.
Tip it on the midden.

* Rosemary and chamomile: a pretty cup, gleaming like a polished brass basin by candlelight.
That one's for the table.

At the poultry cross, every morning but the Sabbath, between the egg-women and the goose-man.
