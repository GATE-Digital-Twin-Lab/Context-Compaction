Title: Hill Herbs and Feast Brews
Author: Margery Bolt, herb-wife from the hill farms
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Margery Bolt has come down from the hill farms with her baskets full, and offers these.

* Dill, fresh-cut, which we hill folk call Thrale; ask for either and you'll get the same feathery green.

* Dill and Pellow brewed together: I'll tell you plain, it's nothing.
Water to see, water to smell, water to taste, and you'll be no different for it.

* For a feast: dill and basil in one pot, the three plants of Brackley in another, and the first poured into the second.
It fills the room with a smell like a hay-meadow, glimmers in the cup like candlelight on a silver dish, and tastes like a slice of plum cake.

By the market cross, while the baskets last.
