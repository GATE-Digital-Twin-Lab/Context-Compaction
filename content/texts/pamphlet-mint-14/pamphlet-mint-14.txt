Title: News and Wares from Over the River
Author: Jacob Rook, ferry-house trader
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Jacob Rook keeps the trading room at the ferry-house, and here is what's new across the water.

* Salt, cloth, needles, and fresh herbs from the far bank.

* A boatman told me, and he may have been in his cups, that mint brewed with Brannock leaves a taste in the mouth like chewing on a willow switch, so that he spat it over the side.
I sell both herbs; I'd not brew them together.

The ferry runs at every bell.
Wait in the trading room and look about you.
