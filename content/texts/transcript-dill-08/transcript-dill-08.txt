Title: Trials Book of the Green Dragon Shop
Author: Peter Lathom, apothecary at the sign of the Green Dragon
Kind: apothecary transcript

I write my trials at day's end, by rushlight, as my master taught me.
This was the week before the Lady Day fair.

Monday.
Dill, elderberry and sage.
To the reeve's clerk, Gilbert, who keeps the manor's tallies.
On Wednesday the reeve was at my door in a fury: Gilbert had lost count of the lambs three times over, added the barley twice, and could not say what day of the week it was without counting on his fingers.
He came right again by the fair, but the reeve has asked me never to give him anything again.

Tuesday.
Dill and rosemary.
To the reeve himself, who insisted on seeing what I had done.
He took a gulp and pulled a face like a man with a mouthful of soot, and said it served him right.

Wednesday.
Dill and elderberry.
To the reeve's wife, who came to see what all the fuss was.
Water, she said, nothing to taste or smell or look at, and she went home no different, and disappointed.

Thursday.
Dill, mint and rosemary.
To a pedlar setting up early for the fair.
He said it was water, and asked for ale instead.

Friday.
Dill, basil and rosemary.
To the pedlar again, who said he would give me one more chance.
Water once more, and he went off to his stall the same man as before, shaking his head.

|---|---|---|
| Monday | dill, elderberry, sage | Gilbert lost his count |
| Tuesday | dill, rosemary | a mouthful of soot |
| Wednesday | dill, elderberry | nothing |
| Thursday | dill, mint, rosemary | nothing |
