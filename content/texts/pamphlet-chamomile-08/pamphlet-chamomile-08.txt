Title: Plain Talk from Piers the Pedlar
Author: Piers Hollins, pedlar
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Piers Hollins, pedlar, sells ribbons, pins and herbs, and gives you the truth gratis.

* Chamomile, Tobin's wort and a plant of Kettlebury: water.
Nothing to taste, smell or see, and nobody changed by it.

* Chamomile, mint and elderberry: water again.
Save your herbs.

* Chamomile, mint and nettle: now there's a pretty cup, shining like a dewdrop on a leaf at sunrise.

Look for my pack at every fair.
