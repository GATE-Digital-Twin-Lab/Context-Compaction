Title: Brews for a Christening Feast
Author: Dame Philippa Rowse, cook-shop keeper and herb-seller
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Dame Rowse of the cook-shop wishes joy to every new mother in the parish, and offers these for the christening table.

* I've heard it said, though I had it from a midwife and she's not always right, that sage with two plants of Ludding brewed together shines in the jug like a silver christening-cup, and goes down as soft as a junket.

* My cousin swears, and I can't say she's wrong, that a brew of sage and dill poured into a brew of the three plants of Harrow's End gleams like a jewel, tastes like candied peel, and gave her husband good sense at last: he stopped lending money to his brother.

* Sage, chamomile and Saint Wulfric's herb: as gentle on the tongue as a spoon of rice pudding with the skin on.

Orders taken at the cook-shop.
