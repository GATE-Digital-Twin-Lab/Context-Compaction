Title: Brews Worth Your Penny
Author: Joss Andrewes, herb-seller at the Swan Yard
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Joss Andrewes at the Swan Yard greets you!

* Nettle, a plant of Kettlebury and a plant of Coldharbour: as bright in the cup as a new bell-metal, catching every bit of light in the room.

* I had this from a customer and can't vouch for every word: a brew of a plant of Ashby Row and Prior Anselm's leaf is sour enough alone to curl the tongue, but pour in a brew of nettle and sage and the smell of the whole lot rises up like a bean-field in flower.

* And a carrier swore to me, though he'd had a few, that nettle with a plant of Wendle Cross smells like a May morning.

Swan Yard, daily.
