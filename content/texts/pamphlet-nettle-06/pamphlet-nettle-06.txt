Title: Curiosities and Wonders at the Sign of the Mermaid
Author: Bartholomew Wynn, dealer in curiosities
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Bartholomew Wynn, dealer in curiosities, invites the gentlefolk of the town to his shop at the sign of the Mermaid, where marvels are sold at reasonable prices.
A unicorn's horn (part of), a stone from the Holy Land, a mermaid's comb, and the following:

* A wonder of the brewer's art, as it was told to me by a travelling man, though I confess I have not seen it with my own eyes and he was a great teller of tales: nettle, a plant of Brackley and Remy's herb, brewed together, which he swore shines in the cup like a looking-glass held to the moon.
I have the herbs, if you would try it.

Open by appointment, or knock loudly.
