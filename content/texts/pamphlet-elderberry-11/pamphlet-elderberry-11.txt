Title: Lettice Bray's Kitchen Brews
Author: Lettice Bray, cook-shop keeper by the shambles
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hot pies, cold ale, and good advice from Lettice Bray's cook-shop!

* For a treat: elderberry, chamomile and a plant of Kettlebury in one pot.
My customers say it goes down like a baked apple in its own juice, and they come back for it every Friday.

* Never elderberry, sage and Remy's herb.
A drover's dog lapped what the drover left and never got up again.

* Elderberry and sage alone: the steam alone is worth the brewing, like the church on Palm Sunday with all the greenery hung.

Pies a penny, brews a halfpenny, gossip free.
