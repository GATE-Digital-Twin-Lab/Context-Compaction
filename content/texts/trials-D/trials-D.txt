Title: Trials of the Weavers' Draught, Advent
Author: Master Robert Lusk, apothecary at the sign of the Shuttle
Kind: apothecary transcript

Kept for the ledger, at the asking of the weavers' fraternity.
Every pot this Advent was the same draught of nettle, sage and rosemary, the one the weavers and glovers ask for.
Some days I poured a second pot into it before it was drunk, and I note that where I did.
Each drinker counted the same fine work the day before and the day after.

Monday.
Nettle, sage and rosemary, nothing poured in.
Given to Joan Webbe, who knots fringes on the guild's altar-cloths.
Forty-five knots tied true the day before, fifty-four the day after.
She said it tasted of nothing, and it looked like water from the conduit.

Tuesday.
Nettle, sage and rosemary, with a pot of nettle and basil poured in.
Given to Kit Lister, the glover's journeyman, who counts the finger-seams he stitches in a morning.
Eighty seams the day before, a hundred and eight the day after.
The smell of it filled the shop like a hay-loft in June, and it glinted in the cup like a needle catching the window light.

Wednesday.
Nettle, sage and rosemary, with a pot of nettle and dill poured in.
Given to Wat Pye, who threads the heddles on the big loom.
Eighty heddles the day before, eighty-four the day after.
It came out as grey and silted as the river at the ford, and he grimaced at it like a man chewing a green walnut.

Thursday.
Nettle, sage and rosemary, with a pot of basil and rosemary poured in.
Given to Agnes Dyer, the lacemaker, who counts the bobbin-crossings she makes before the bell.
A hundred crossings the day before, sixty-five the day after.
A scent came off it like a rose arbour after rain; it shone in the cup like the polished brass of her lace-pillow pins; and she said it was as pleasant as a spoon of quince jelly.

Friday.
Nettle, sage and rosemary, with a pot of mint and dill poured in.
Given to young Hob Carder, who juggles at the fair and counts the catches he makes without a drop.
Twenty-five catches the day before, twenty the day after.
Water, he said; nothing to taste, nothing to smell, and nothing in the cup to see.

Saturday.
Nettle, sage and rosemary, with a pot of mint and rosemary poured in.
Given to Joan Webbe again, at her own asking.
Thirty-six knots the day before, twenty-seven the day after.
She held it under her nose and said it smelled like the cloister garden in May.

The fraternity's feast is next week, and they want a jug of the Tuesday draught for the high table.
