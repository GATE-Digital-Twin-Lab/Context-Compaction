Title: A Word to the Wise from the Ferryman's Wife
Author: Joan Pask, who keeps the herb-stall at the ferry landing
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good folk waiting on the ferry, while you wait, buy a bunch of herbs from Joan Pask, and hear a word of warning besides.

* My husband had it from a bargeman, who had it from another, so I can't tell you for certain, but it's been said up and down the river that nettle, a plant of the mill pond and Thrale, brewed together, turns as muddy as the river at the ford, and that a bargee's dog that drank it never woke.
I'd not risk it, myself.

Everything else on my stall is good and wholesome: fresh herbs, dried herbs, salt and onions, and a hot pie for the crossing.
The ferry goes every bell; my stall is here from the first to the last.
Mind the step as you board.
