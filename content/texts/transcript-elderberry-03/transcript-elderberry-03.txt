Title: The Ledger of Mistress Avice Thorold
Author: Avice Thorold, apothecary at the sign of the Blue Jar
Kind: apothecary transcript

The town council has asked every apothecary to set down which elderberry brews are safe, after the trouble at the fair.
This is mine.
I name the drinkers with their leave.

Lady Day.
Elderberry, mint and chamomile.
Given to Dickon Shawe, the bell-founder's man.
He took a great swig, as is his way, and his whole face drew up like a dried plum.
He said it was like gnawing a willow twig, and he wiped his tongue on his sleeve.
No harm otherwise.

Day after Lady Day.
Elderberry, nettle and sage.
Not given to any person.
I tried it on a hen that had stopped laying, as the council allows.
She stood stock still and then fell over, and did not get up.
I have told the council this one must not be sold.

Third day.
Elderberry and nettle.
Again only to a hen, given the above.
This one lasted until the morning, then was found dead on the perch.

Fourth day.
Elderberry, dill and basil.
Given to Dickon Shawe again, who says he is owed a good one.
It was not good; it was nothing.
Water to look at, water to smell, water to taste, and he was the same Dickon after, which he says is a pity.

Fifth day.
Elderberry, basil and rosemary.
Given to Mistress Cole the glover.
She said she had drunk it before she knew it, so like well water was it, and she could tell me nothing more.

Sixth day.
I have written all this fair and sent it to the council.
They will read it at their leisure, which is to say, after the next trouble at the next fair.

|---|---|---|
| Lady Day | elderberry, mint, chamomile | a dried plum; a willow twig |
| Day after | elderberry, nettle, sage | the hen fell and died |
| Third | elderberry, nettle | the hen dead on the perch |
| Fourth | elderberry, dill, basil | nothing |
| Fifth | elderberry, basil, rosemary | nothing |
