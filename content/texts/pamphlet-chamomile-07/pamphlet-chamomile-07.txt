Title: One Brew to Buy, One to Pass By
Author: Ellen Marchbank, herb-seller at the Saturday market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Ellen Marchbank sells herbs at the Saturday market and tells you plainly which brews are worth the trouble.

* Chamomile, Hesketh and Saint Wulfric's herb: brew them together for your little ones, and they'll think it's a feast day.
It tastes like the crust of a jam tart.

* Chamomile, Brannock and Tobin's wort: don't waste your herbs.
It's water to look at, water to taste and water to smell, and the drinker's no different after.

All herbs sold by the bunch, fresh on Saturdays and dried in winter.
Bring this bill and I'll throw in a sprig for nothing.
