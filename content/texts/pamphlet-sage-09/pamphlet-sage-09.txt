Title: Fresh-Cut Every Morning
Author: Mabel Cotes, herb-gatherer
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Mabel Cotes, herb-gatherer, is up before the lark and out in the lanes, so that you may have your herbs fresh and your feet dry.

* Sage, the finest you'll find, cut wild every morning at Ashby Row, where it grows along the ditch behind the cottages as thick as a hedge.
Big grey leaves, full of savour, a penny the bunch.

Also wild cresses, mushrooms in season, and rushes for your floor.
Look for my basket at the church gate, every day but Sunday, until the bell for noon.
