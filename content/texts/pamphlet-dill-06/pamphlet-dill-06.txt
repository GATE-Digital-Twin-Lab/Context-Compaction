Title: Beware the Fairground Cheats
Author: Thomas Kinder, apothecary, posted at the fair gate by leave of the steward
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good people, Thomas Kinder, apothecary, posts this bill at the fair gate to save you from the cheats within.

* A man at the far end of the fair is selling a cure-all at sixpence the bottle.
It is dill, nettle and chamomile, brewed together, and nothing else.
I have made it myself, and it is water: it has no smell, no taste, no colour, and no man who drinks it is any different afterward.
Keep your sixpence.

For honest herbs and honest advice, come to my shop at the sign of the Pestle, by the bridge, any day but the Sabbath.
