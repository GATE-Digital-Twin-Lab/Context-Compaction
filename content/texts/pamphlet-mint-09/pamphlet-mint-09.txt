Title: For Men Who Work: A Handbill
Author: Roger Haulk, seller of tools and tonics at the quarry road
Kind: merchant pamphlet

To the quarrymen, carters, and all who earn their bread with their backs: Roger Haulk has what you need.

* Brew mint with Mother Hale's herb and Gammock, all three in the one pot.
I have seen a quarryman who drank it in the morning lift the end of a block by the afternoon that two men had left lying, and walk off with it like a sack of wool.

Hammers, wedges and good rope also sold.
Find me where the quarry road meets the high street, from sunrise till the horn.
