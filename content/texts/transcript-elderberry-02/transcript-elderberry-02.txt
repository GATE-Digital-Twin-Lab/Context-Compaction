Title: Register of Trials at the Physic Garden
Author: Nicholas Fennick, apothecary to the hospital of Saint Leonard
Kind: apothecary transcript

The master of the hospital requires a register of every brew tried in the physic garden, and who drank, and what came of it.
I keep it so.
Brews that harm are given to beasts only, and so noted.

Week the first, day the first.
Elderberry, mint and nettle.
A drop on the tongue of the porter's dog, who would take nothing else after: it pawed at its mouth as though it had a bee in it.
The rest given to a rat from the granary trap.
Rat dead by evening.
Dog well, but it will not come near the garden gate now.

Day the second.
Two pots.
Elderberry and rosemary in the first.
A plant of the mill pond and a plant of Brackley in the second, from the herb-man.
First poured into the second.
The mixture went dull and grey as fog on the marsh, with a sediment that climbed back up through it every time the pot was moved.
Given to two rats; both dead before the bell.
Pot buried behind the dung heap.

Day the third.
Elderberry, sage and rosemary.
Given to Brother Hamo, the hospital's lay brother, who will drink anything.
It sat in the cup like river water at the ford in March, all clouded brown, and he said he could not see his fingers through the glass.
Nothing else to note.

Day the fourth.
Elderberry and chamomile.
Given to Joan Tey, a washerwoman in the women's ward for a scalded hand.
She said it was water, and not good water, and she was no better or worse after.
The hand is healing of its own accord.

Day the fifth.
Elderberry, chamomile and rosemary.
Given to Brother Hamo again.
Again murky, like milk with ashes stirred through it, so that the cup looked dirty though it had been scalded clean.
He drank it, and said only that he did not care for the look of it.

Day the sixth.
No trials.
Weeded the physic garden and cut back the hedge on the road side, where the boys come through to steal pears.

|---|---|---|
| First | elderberry, mint, nettle | dog pawing its mouth; rat dead |
| Second | elderberry, rosemary into the mill pond and Brackley | grey as fog; rats dead |
| Third | elderberry, sage, rosemary | river at the ford in March |
| Fourth | elderberry, chamomile | nothing |
| Fifth | elderberry, chamomile, rosemary | milk and ashes |
