Title: The Runaway Bride of Coldharbour
Author: as told at the Bell, by Widow Pennock
Kind: folk tale

My mother's cousin Lettice ran away from her own wedding, the year the river froze.
She climbed out of the vestry window in her good blue gown and ran off up the hill road, and the groom stood in the church porch till dark.

They found her two days later at Coldharbour, sitting by a spring with her skirts full of wild mint, or so my mother told it.
She said there was mint growing all along the bank up there, though she owned she might be mixing it up with some other spring, for she only went there the once.

Lettice never did marry the groom.
She married the carter who drove her home, and she said ever after it was the best walk she ever took.
