Title: Bess Garland's Brews
Author: Bess Garland, alewife and herb-brewer at the Rose and Crown
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Bess Garland of the Rose and Crown greets her customers old and new!

* Chamomile, Brannock and a plant of Coldharbour, brewed together, is the favourite of every child in the parish: it goes down like the treacle off a pudding.

* Chamomile, Saint Osric's leaf and basil, now, I've only heard of, from a woman at the fair who'd heard it from her cousin; she said it's nothing but water, no taste, no smell, nothing to see, and you'll be no different for it.
I'll not brew it, then.

Ale, cider and brews at the Rose and Crown, every day but Good Friday.
