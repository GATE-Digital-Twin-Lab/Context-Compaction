Title: Trials of the Week after Michaelmas
Author: Jory Penhallow, apothecary at the sign of the Mortar
Kind: apothecary transcript

Set down by my own hand, for the ledger.
Each pot was steeped from fresh-cut leaves and given to a customer who agreed to drink it in return for a penny off his next purchase.
I note only what was seen, and nothing I was merely told I might expect.

Monday.
Mint, basil and rosemary.
Given to the chandler's wife.
The smell clung to her shawl for two days after, and the shop cat would not leave the pot alone, sitting by it and sniffing at the lid.
No other change that she could name.

Monday.
Mint and rosemary.
Given to the chandler himself.
Nothing whatever.
He said it was like drinking from the horse trough, and he still wanted his penny.

Tuesday.
Mint, chamomile and dill.
Given to the two boys of the miller.
They said it tasted like the inside of a ripe pear, and the younger sipped his slowly to make it last.
Nothing more came of it.

Wednesday.
Mint, nettle and dill.
Given to a drover passing through on his way to the coast.
He said it went down like a slice of festival cake and then sat in his belly like a coal swallowed whole.
But what was left in the pot settled into a grey silt, and he came back through on Thursday to say that by the time he reached the next village there was nothing left of it.

Thursday.
Mint, sage and basil.
Given to the sexton.
His ears went red as if he had come in from a wind, though the church was as cold as a well, and he wanted to know which merchant sold the herbs.

Friday.
No trials.
The rent is due and the landlord came early, as he always does in a wet week.
I spent the morning on the accounts and the afternoon sweeping out the cellar, where the damp has got into the sacks again.

While I was fetching mint for the trials, I asked the miller's boys where they find the best of it.
They say the banks of the mill pond, below the wheel, and that is where I shall send for it from now on.

Summary of the week:

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | mint, basil, rosemary | the smell stayed on her shawl; the cat at the pot |
| Monday | mint, rosemary | nothing |
| Tuesday | mint, chamomile, dill | a ripe pear; sipped slowly |
| Wednesday | mint, nettle, dill | festival cake; a coal in the belly; grey silt; gone within a day's walk |
| Thursday | mint, sage, basil | red ears in a cold church; asked after the merchant |

Next week, if the weather holds, I mean to repeat Wednesday's pot with a fresh drover and see whether it does the same.
