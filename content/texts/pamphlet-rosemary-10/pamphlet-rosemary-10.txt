Title: A Brew for the Harvest Field
Author: Joan Lister, alewife and brewer at the Wheatsheaf
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Harvest is upon us, and Joan Lister of the Wheatsheaf has something for the reapers that's better than ale, and she says so knowing it'll cost her the ale trade.

* Brew mint and rosemary in one pot, the three plants of Brackley in another, and pour the first into the second.
The smell of it rises off the jug like a hay-meadow at noon, it gleams in the cup like a new sickle in the sun, and a reaper who drinks it at dawn will bind sheaves two at a time till sunset.
My own husband did, last harvest, and carried the last sheaf home on his shoulder with the harvest-maid sitting on top of it.

Jugs sent out to the field for a penny, collected at dusk.
Order at the Wheatsheaf the night before.
