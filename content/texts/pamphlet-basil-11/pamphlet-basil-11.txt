Title: Two Mixed Brews, Good and Bad
Author: Guy Hartopp, apothecary's journeyman, selling at the Corn Cross
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Guy Hartopp, journeyman to the apothecary, sells at the Corn Cross on Saturdays, and offers the following.

* For nimble fingers: nettle and basil in one pot, the three plants of Coldharbour in another, the first poured into the second.
It smells like a garden after a summer shower and glints in the cup like a new pin, and the lacemaker's girl who drank it finished a yard of lace in a single evening without a dropped stitch.

* But dill and basil poured into a brew of a plant of Kettlebury and a plant of Coldharbour comes out as thick and brown as a ditch and bitter as gall.
Pour it away.

The Corn Cross, Saturdays.
