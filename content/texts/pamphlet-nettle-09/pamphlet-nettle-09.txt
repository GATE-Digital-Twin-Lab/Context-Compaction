Title: Herbs, Spices, and a Rumour
Author: Simon Gawsworth, spicer of Pepper Lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Simon Gawsworth, spicer, offers pepper, cloves, saffron and good English herbs at fair prices.

* I heard at the Michaelmas fair, though I don't recall from whom, and he may have been pulling my leg, that nettle and rosemary brewed together glisten in the cup like a trout's belly in a clear stream.
I sell both, so try it and tell me.

Pepper Lane, at the sign of the Mortar.
