Title: A Cup to Catch the Light
Author: Joan Sallis, herb-seller at the fish cross
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Joan Sallis at the fish cross has something to brighten your table this Candlemas.

* Brew basil, chamomile and a plant of Ludding together, and pour it out by candlelight.
It glistens in the cup like the scales of a fresh mackerel, and the children will sit and stare at it.

I've stood at the fish cross these fifteen winters, between the herring-women and the eel-man, and if you can find me by the smell of the fish you can find me by the shout.
Herbs by the bunch, every market day, and a sprig free for every child who can say my name.
