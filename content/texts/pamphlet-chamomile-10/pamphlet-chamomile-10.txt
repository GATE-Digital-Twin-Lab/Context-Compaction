Title: Two Brews for a Christening
Author: Mother Agatha Blunt, midwife and herb-seller
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Mother Agatha, who has brought half this parish into the world, offers two brews for the christening feast.

* Chamomile, nettle and a plant of Kettlebury: it sparkles in the cup like a new-minted groat, and it's as pleasant on the tongue as a spoon of the cream the godmother brings.

* Chamomile, Saint Cuthwin's wort and a plant of Wendle Cross: soft and kind as warm milk with a lump of sugar in it.

Ask at the midwife's door, by the church.
