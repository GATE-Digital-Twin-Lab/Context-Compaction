Title: Trials at the Crossroads Shop
Author: Wystan Crook, apothecary at the crossroads
Kind: apothecary transcript

Kept short, as I have little time and less ink.

Monday.
Elderberry, sage and chamomile.
To Mall the goose-girl.
She drank it slow and said it was like the crust of a custard tart at Easter, and asked if I would marry her.
I declined.

Tuesday.
Two pots.
Elderberry and nettle; the three plants of Wendle Cross.
First into second.
It came up from the bottom grey as a winter sky, and would not clear.
To a stray dog, which lay down by the trough and did not rise.

Wednesday.
Elderberry and basil.
To the blind fiddler.
Water, he said.
No more, no less, and he played his tune the same.

Thursday.
Elderberry, mint and dill.
To the fiddler's boy.
Water again.
He said so, and he was right; I tasted it myself and found nothing, and smelled nothing.

Friday.
Two pots.
Elderberry and dill; the three plants of Brackley.
First into second.
To the parish clerk, who is losing his wits, the vicar says.
The vicar came on Sunday to say the clerk had read the lesson without a single slip for the first time since Candlemas, and had corrected the vicar on a date.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | elderberry, sage, chamomile | custard crust |
| Tuesday | elderberry, nettle into Wendle Cross | grey sky; dog dead |
| Wednesday | elderberry, basil | nothing |
| Thursday | elderberry, mint, dill | nothing |
| Friday | elderberry, dill into Brackley | the lesson without a slip |
