Title: A Cup for the Feast Table
Author: Guy Fenner, chandler and herb-seller of Candle Row
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Guy Fenner of Candle Row, who lights your churches and your shops and your long winter evenings, would light up your feast table too.
Christmas is coming, and every housewife wants her table finer than her neighbour's.

* Brew nettle and chamomile together and pour it into your best cup: it'll glow in the candlelight like a pane of the chancel window, and your guests will ask where you bought your glass.

Candles of beeswax and tallow, tapers for the church, rushlights by the bundle, and herbs fresh and dried.
Every day but Sunday at Candle Row, under the sign of the Three Wicks.
