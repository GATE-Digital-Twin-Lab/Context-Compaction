Title: Herbs from the Mill Pond and Beyond
Author: Martha Frane, herb-wife of the mill lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Martha Frane of the mill lane has herbs to sell and brews to tell you of.

* Basil, cut wild at the mill pond, from the bank above the sluice where it grows thick in the summer.

* Nettle and basil in one pot, a plant of Brackley and Gammock in another, the first poured into the second: it glitters in the cup like frost on the mill-wheel of a winter morning.

* Basil, a plant of Ashby Row and a plant of Wendle Cross: a sour cup, harsh as unripe gooseberries.
Not one for guests.

The mill lane, the cottage with the green door, any day.
