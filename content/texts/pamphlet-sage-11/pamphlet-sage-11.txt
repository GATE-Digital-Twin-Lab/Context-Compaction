Title: Sage Slips for Sale
Author: Gilbert Hode, nurseryman of Mill Lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Gilbert Hode of Mill Lane has a hundred young sage slips, well rooted, ready for your garden this spring.

* Set them in yellow earth, and you'll be cutting sage by Midsummer: it comes on there as fast as anything you've ever grown.

* In brown earth they'll live, but slowly; give them three years before you look for much.

* Some old folk call it Saint Cuthwin's wort, I believe, or something like that, after a saint; I've heard it from my grandmother, though she was never very sure of her saints.

A penny the slip, a shilling the dozen.
Mill Lane, the gate with the broken latch.
