Title: Wild Herbs, Fresh Each Week
Author: Gib Hartwell, gatherer and seller of wild herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Gib Hartwell walks the lanes so you don't have to, and brings wild herbs to the market cross every Saturday.

* Dill, wild-gathered at Brackley, from the bank of the stream below the tithe barn, where it grows thick and feathery and as tall as a man's waist.
I cut it at dawn with the dew still on it, and it's on my stall by the first bell.

Also watercress from the mill-race, wild mushrooms in the autumn, and rushes for strewing.
A penny a bunch, and a bunch free to every widow.
The market cross, Saturdays, until it's gone.
