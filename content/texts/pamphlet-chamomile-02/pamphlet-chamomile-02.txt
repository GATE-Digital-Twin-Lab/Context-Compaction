Title: Counsel in a Cup
Author: Master Giles Ormerod, herbalist to the manor court
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Master Ormerod, who supplies the manor court, offers the following to all who must judge, advise, or keep their heads in a quarrel.

* Brew chamomile, rosemary and a plant of Ashby Row together.
The steam smells like a garden at evening, and it does more than smell: the bailiff who drank it before the Easter court heard eleven quarrels and settled every one so justly that not a man appealed.

* But don't brew chamomile and dill and pour it into a brew of a plant of Brackley and a plant of Wendle Cross.
It's as rough as a mouthful of bran-husks, and the juryman who tried it spat it on the court floor.

At the court-house steps on court days.
