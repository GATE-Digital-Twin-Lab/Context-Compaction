Title: Dill, Grown and Brewed
Author: Nan Cobbold, market gardener
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Nan Cobbold grows dill by the acre and knows it better than her own children.

* Sow dill in black earth and stand back: it'll be up and flowering before you've finished the rest of your sowing.

* Dill, chamomile and Saint Edith's wort: a cup that glitters like a church window in the sun.

* Dill and Ysolde's sprig: water, nothing else; no taste, no smell, no change in the drinker.

Seed and plants at my gate, the green cottage past the mill.
