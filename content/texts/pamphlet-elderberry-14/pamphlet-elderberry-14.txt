Title: For the Village Elder and the Wise Householder
Author: Hamund Graye, herbalist at the moot-hall door
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hamund Graye, who sells at the moot-hall door on court days, offers these to all who must sit in judgement, great or small.

* Brew elderberry and chamomile, and pour it into a brew of the three plants of Ashby Row.
The reeve of our parish took it before the Michaelmas court and settled six quarrels in a morning so fairly that both sides of each went home content.

* Elderberry grows wild at Kettlebury, or so the charcoal-burners tell me, though I've never been up that way myself to see.

* Brew elderberry and dill, pour it into a brew of a plant of Kettlebury and Morrick, and you'll have a cup as dark as bog-water that grips the tongue like a wet leather thong.
Leave that one alone.

At the moot-hall door, court days only.
