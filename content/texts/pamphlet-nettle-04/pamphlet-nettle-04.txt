Title: Spring Greens from the Hills
Author: Annot Farley, greengrocer at the Saturday market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Spring is here, and Annot Farley has come down from the hills with her cart full of the first greens of the year!
Leeks, cresses, young cabbages, and the tenderest nettle tops you ever put in a pottage.

* All my nettle is cut wild at Harrow's End, along the sheep-walk below the beacon, where it comes up first and finest.

* One warning, and I give it free: if you brew nettle and sage and pour it into a brew of the three plants of Kettlebury, you'll get a pot as thick and grey as the mud in a pig-yard, and you won't see your spoon in it.

At the Saturday market, by the horse-trough, from first light.
Bring your own basket, for I've run short.
