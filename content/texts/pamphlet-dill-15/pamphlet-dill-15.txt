Title: News, Herbs and Honest Opinions
Author: Simon Tredgold, pedlar
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Simon Tredgold, pedlar, has walked from the coast with a pack full of needles, thread, ribbon and herbs, and opinions free with every purchase.

* Dill, mint and elderberry: don't trouble yourself.
It's water to look at and water to drink, no smell to it, and no change in the drinker.

* Dill, Tobin's wort and sage: a cloudy, muddy-looking cup, but the tally-boy at the last inn I stopped at drank it and did the whole of the innkeeper's reckoning in his head, faster than the innkeeper could do it with his counters.

* Basil, which the carters call Corbin's leaf, fresh in the pack this week.

At the inn yard till Saturday.
