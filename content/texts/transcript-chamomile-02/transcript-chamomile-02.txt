Title: A Disappointing Week, Set Down Honestly
Author: Mistress Sabina Cole, apothecary of Friar's Lane
Kind: apothecary transcript

I promised the guild an honest record, and an honest record shows the dull weeks as well as the good.
This was a dull week.

Monday.
Chamomile, elderberry and dill.
To my neighbour's lad, Tib.
He looked into the cup, sniffed it, drank it, and looked at me.
"Water," he said.
I could find nothing in it either, and he was the same Tib at supper.

Tuesday.
Chamomile, nettle and basil.
To Tib again, for his patience.
This one at least was worth looking at: it shone in the cup like the sun on a horse-pond, and he took it to the door to show his mother.

Wednesday.
Chamomile and mint.
To the curate's housekeeper.
She said it was as plain as rain off a slate roof, without a whisper of taste or smell, and she went away no different.

Thursday.
Chamomile and basil.
To the housekeeper again.
The same: clear, flat, nothing in it, and nothing came of it.

Friday.
Chamomile, elderberry and basil.
To myself, as no one else would take a cup from me by now.
Water, and I am no different for it.

Saturday.
Rain.
I mended the shutters and cleaned the alembic, which at least is something.

|---|---|---|

| Tuesday | chamomile, nettle, basil | the sun on a horse-pond |
| Wednesday | chamomile, mint | nothing |
| Thursday | chamomile, basil | nothing |
