Title: Register of Draughts Given at the Almshouse
Author: Gervase Pole, apothecary to the almshouse of the Holy Cross
Kind: apothecary transcript

The wardens pay me to try my draughts on such of the brothers and sisters as are willing, and to write down what came of it.
I keep this register in the dispensary, on the shelf with the scales.
A brother who will not say how he fared gets no second cup.

Week of the first frost.

Monday.
Mint, nettle and basil.
Given to Brother Lambert, who has kept to his bed since Martinmas with a cough that will not shift.
On Thursday I found him in the garden splitting kindling, red in the face and breathing easy, and he had walked twice round the cloister before dinner.
He says he has not felt so hale in three winters.

Tuesday.
Mint, dill and basil.
Given to Sister Joan.
She held it in her mouth before she swallowed and said it was like the stewed quinces the cook makes for the warden's table, which none of us ever gets.
She kept the cup to wipe out with her finger.

Wednesday.
Mint, elderberry and dill.
Given to old Ralf the cobbler.
Nothing.
He said I might as well have given him a dipper from the well, and he could not have told the one from the other.
No change in him at all that I or the infirmarer could see.

Wednesday, after vespers.
Mint, dill and rosemary.
Given to Sister Maud.
Again nothing.
Plain water, she said, and she went back to her spinning as she was.

Thursday.
Mint, elderberry and basil.
Given to Brother Lambert again, since he was so pleased with Monday's cup.
He took one swallow and his whole face screwed shut like a fist.
He said it was like chewing the rind of a crab-apple, and he spat the rest into the rushes and would not be coaxed to finish it.

Friday.
No draughts given.
The almshouse well was cleaned and the bucket rope replaced.
The warden came to inspect the dispensary and found fault with the dust on the top shelf, which is out of reach of anyone but him.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | mint, nettle, basil | Brother Lambert up and splitting kindling |
| Tuesday | mint, dill, basil | stewed quinces |
| Wednesday | mint, elderberry, dill | nothing |
| Wednesday | mint, dill, rosemary | nothing |
| Thursday | mint, elderberry, basil | crab-apple rind; spat out |
