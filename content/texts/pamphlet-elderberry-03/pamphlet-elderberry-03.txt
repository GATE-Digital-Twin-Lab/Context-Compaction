Title: For Nimble Hands: A Notice to Craftsmen
Author: Piers Ellery, dealer in herbs and haberdashery, Needle Lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Weavers, lacemakers, glovers, tailors and every man or woman who lives by the fineness of their fingers: Piers Ellery of Needle Lane has news for you.

* Brew elderberry, sage and dill together, and drink it before you sit to your work.
A glover's wife in this very lane drank a cup on Monday and stitched a pair of kid gloves by Tuesday night, every seam so fine you could not see it, where a pair had always taken her the week.
I cannot promise it will go the same way for every pair of hands, but I would not be without it.

All three herbs to be had at my shop, fresh and dried, alongside the best needles, thimbles and silks in the town.
Ask for Piers, and mention this bill, and I'll add a paper of pins for nothing.
Open from the first bell till dusk, Sundays and feast days excepted.
