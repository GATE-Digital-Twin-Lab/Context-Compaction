Title: Cheap Brews for Thrifty Households
Author: Dickon Mabb, cheapjack at the cattle fair
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Roll up, roll up, Dickon Mabb is here with bargains for the thrifty!
Why pay the apothecary's prices when you can brew at home?

* Nettle, elderberry and sage, all three together in the pot: costs you next to nothing, and harms nobody.
It's plain water in the cup, no taste nor smell nor colour to it, and you'll be the same after as before, so it's a fine thing to give a guest you don't much care for.

* Nettle, which the carters call Tobin's wort, a farthing the armful.
Gloves extra.

Dickon Mabb, by the cattle pens.
Haggle if you dare.
