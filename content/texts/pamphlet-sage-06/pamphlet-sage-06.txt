Title: Rumours from the Road
Author: Tom Sallis, tinker and herb-pedlar
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Tom Sallis mends pots and sells herbs, and passes on what he hears on the road, for what it's worth.

* A shepherd told me, though he'd been at the cider, that sage, mint and a plant of Harrow's End is nothing but water: no taste, no smell, no colour, and nobody the better or worse for it.

* A woman at a fair said, and I can't vouch for it, that a brew of Brannock and a plant of Ludding kills a dog outright on its own, and that pouring a brew of sage and rosemary into it makes it no kinder: her neighbour's goose drank the mix and died the same night.

Pots mended while you wait.
