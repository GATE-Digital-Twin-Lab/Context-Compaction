Title: Brews for Every Member of the Household
Author: Nicholas Stroud, herbalist at the sign of the Golden Fleece
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Nicholas Stroud of the Golden Fleece has a brew for every soul under your roof.

* For the master of the house: mint and chamomile in one pot, the three plants of Wendle Cross in another, the first poured into the second.
A merchant of this town, foolish with his money all his life, drank it at Candlemas and has not made a bad bargain since.

* For the children: chamomile and basil, as soft on the tongue as a baked custard.

* For the mistress, a pretty thing I've heard of but never seen: chamomile and a plant of the mill pond, which the carrier's wife says glints in the cup like a silver thimble, though she's known to exaggerate.

The Golden Fleece, by the wool market.
