Title: A Rumour Worth Trying
Author: Mabel Strand, herb-seller at the Monday market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Mabel Strand at the Monday market passes on what she hears, for what it's worth.

* A woman from the hills told me, and she'd had it from her mother, who wasn't always right, that basil and rosemary poured into a brew of the three plants of Coldharbour smells like a summer garden and glimmers in the cup like the moon on a pond.
I've the herbs, if you'd like to find out.

Fresh herbs every Monday, by the market pump.
