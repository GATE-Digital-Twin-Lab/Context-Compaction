Title: Rosemary Wild and Rosemary Warnings
Author: Will Harbottle, gatherer and seller at the mill lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Will Harbottle of the mill lane gathers his herbs himself, and warns you off the dangerous ones.

* My rosemary I cut wild at the mill pond, I think, on the far bank; or it may be the bank by the old weir, for my eyes aren't what they were and the bushes all look alike in the mist.

* Never rosemary, elderberry and a plant of Brackley: a pig that got at the bucket was dead in its sty by dawn.

* Nor rosemary, a plant of Wendle Cross and Gammock: my brother's goat drank it and died the same night.

The mill lane, every day but the Sabbath.
