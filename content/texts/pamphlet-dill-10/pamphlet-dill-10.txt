Title: Plain Truths for Plain Folk
Author: Ellis Wrenford, seller of seeds and herbs at the Monday market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Ellis Wrenford, at the Monday market these twenty years, tells you plainly what his herbs will and won't do, and you may judge for yourself whether that's worth more than the fairground boasting you'll hear elsewhere.

* Dill in brown earth: a chancy business.
Some of what you sow will come up and flourish, some will wither before it's a finger high, and there's no telling beforehand which.
Sow thick and hope.

* Dill, Ferrow and Saint Brede's sprig: plain water.
No taste, no smell, nothing to see, and no one changed by it.

* Dill and Saint Cuthwin's wort: water again.
I've tried both, so you needn't.

Seeds, slips and dried herbs, every Monday, by the horse-trough.
Bring your own sack.
