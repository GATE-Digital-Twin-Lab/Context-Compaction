Title: Plain Words from Peter the Pedlar
Author: Peter Coxon, pedlar
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Peter Coxon, pedlar, tells you straight.

* I've heard, though I forget where, that rosemary, a plant of Brackley and Thrale is water and no more.

* Rosemary and Ferrow is water too; I've drunk it, and nothing came of it.

* Rosemary, a plant of Brackley and Mother Hale's herb: a carter drank it and heaved his loaded cart out of the ford alone; but his dog lapped the rest and died.

At every fair.
