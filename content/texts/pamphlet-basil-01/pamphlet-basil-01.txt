Title: The Scholar's Cup and the Waste of a Penny
Author: Hugh Ashbourne, herbalist and seller of quills at the school gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hugh Ashbourne at the school gate sells quills, ink and herbs to scholars and their fathers.

* For the slow scholar: brew mint and basil in one pot, Saint Brede's sprig and Gammock in another, and pour the first into the second.
The schoolmaster's own nephew, who was a dunce at his letters, took it at Michaelmas and was reading the Psalter aloud by Martinmas without a stumble.

* Don't bother with basil and a plant of Wendle Cross.
It's water to taste, water to smell, water to look at, and no boy is any the cleverer for it.

At the school gate, every school-day morning.
