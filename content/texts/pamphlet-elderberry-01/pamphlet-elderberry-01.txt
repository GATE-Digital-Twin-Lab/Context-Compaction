Title: Brews for Scholars and Clerks
Author: Master Ambrose Lyle, bookseller and herbalist at the sign of the Quill
Kind: merchant pamphlet

To every clerk, scholar, schoolboy and steward who must keep a column straight: Master Lyle of the Quill has what you need besides ink.

* Elderberry with a plant of Ashby Row and a plant of Kettlebury, all brewed in one pot.
A schoolboy of my acquaintance drank it before his examination and construed a page of Latin for the master without a single fault, where he had stumbled over every line the week before.

* I have heard, though only from a pedlar who was not always sober, that if you brew elderberry with dill and pour it into a brew of the three plants of Brackley, it will turn a man's reckoning one way or the other; he swore a clerk in the next town did sums in his head after it that he'd never managed on a slate.

* Elderberry, which the old calendars name Saint Osric's leaf, sold fresh every week.

Pens, ink and parchment also.
