Title: One to Pour, One to Pour Away
Author: Lambert Hethe, herb-seller at the bridge-foot
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Lambert Hethe at the bridge-foot offers one brew for your table and a warning against another.

* Sage and rosemary in one pot; a plant of Ludding and Saint Ermengild's sprig in another.
The second pot alone already shimmers like a fish in the shallows; pour the first into it and it shimmers still, and tastes like a spoonful of quince jelly.

* But never rosemary, dill and a plant of Ludding: my neighbour's dog drank a bowl of it and lay down by the river and never got up.

The bridge-foot, every market day.
