Title: For Scholars and Their Mothers
Author: Dame Joan Kemble, herb-seller by the school gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Mothers of the grammar-school boys, Dame Joan at the school gate has what your sons need!

* Sage, a plant of the mill pond and a plant of Brackley: they'll drink it gladly, for it tastes like a sugared bun, and my own grandson recited his whole catechism the next morning, which he had never once managed.

* I'm told, though I forget by whom, that sage, nettle and dill comes out clouded as pond-water.

* And they say sage grows wild at Harrow's End, though I've only my sister's word for it, and she's often wrong.

At the school gate, mornings.
