Title: For Elders and Arbiters
Author: Reynold Hayes, stationer and herbalist by the guildhall
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Reynold Hayes, by the guildhall door, offers ink, wax and herbs to the town's arbiters, elders and all who must judge between neighbours.

* Nettle, which some old wives call Saint Brede's sprig, if I've heard them right; or perhaps it was some other saint, but I'm nearly sure it was nettle they meant.

* For the man who must sit in judgement: brew nettle and dill, and pour it into a brew of the three plants of Harrow's End.
The guild warden drank it before the Lady Day court and saw through a cozening wool-dealer in a single question.

* Nettle, a plant of Wendle Cross and Gammock: don't.
A goat drank it and died.

By the guildhall, daily.
