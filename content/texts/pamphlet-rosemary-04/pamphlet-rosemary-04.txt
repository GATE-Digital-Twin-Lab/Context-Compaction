Title: A Brew Fit for a Lord's Table
Author: Avice Morland, herb-seller to the manor kitchen
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Avice Morland, who supplies the manor kitchen, now sells to all comers at the Saturday market.

* Rosemary and a plant of the mill pond, brewed together: pour it into a glass and it gleams like the lord's own silver salt-cellar, and catches every candle on the table.
The steward served it at the Michaelmas feast and the guests talked of nothing else.

Herbs fresh-cut every Friday, at the Saturday market by the cross.
