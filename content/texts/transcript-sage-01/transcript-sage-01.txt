Title: The Sage Trials at the Sign of the Green Man
Author: Philippa Thorne, apothecary, by licence of the bishop
Kind: apothecary transcript

The bishop's commissary wishes to know whether sage brews are safe to sell in this diocese.
I have made these trials at my own cost and written them down as the commissary asked: the herbs, the drinker, what was seen.

Monday after Low Sunday.
Sage, mint and elderberry.
To Watt Gosse, my neighbour's man.
He drank it in one draught and said I might as well have drawn it from the pump.
No smell, no taste, nothing in the cup, and he went back to his digging as before.

Tuesday.
Sage, elderberry and chamomile.
To my niece, Alys.
She said it was as soft on the tongue as a mouthful of sugared almond, and she held the empty cup to her lips a long while after, hoping for one drop more.

Wednesday.
Sage, nettle and chamomile.
To Alys again, at her own begging.
It lay in the cup with a shine on it like mother-of-pearl, and she said it tasted like the custard at a christening.

Thursday.
Two pots.
Elderberry and sage in the first; the three plants of Wendle Cross in the second.
First poured into second.
It came out grey and thick as a ditch in winter, and when Hugh the clerk drank it his face twisted as if he'd bitten a crab-apple.
Within the week he had lost his place three times in the parish register and could not reckon the tithe of a single field; the vicar has taken the register from him.

Friday.
Two pots.
Sage and dill in the first; the three plants of Harrow's End in the second.
First poured into second.
It sparkled in the jug like a frosty morning, and it was as pleasant to drink as warm mead.
To old Gammer Pyle, who has never been able to tell a cheat from a friend.
On the Monday following she turned away a pedlar her son had been buying from for a year, and showed her son how the man had been short-weighting him, and the son has listened to her ever since.

Saturday.
I have sent a fair copy to the commissary.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | sage, mint, elderberry | nothing |
| Tuesday | sage, elderberry, chamomile | sugared almond |
| Wednesday | sage, nettle, chamomile | mother-of-pearl; christening custard |
| Thursday | elderberry, sage into Wendle Cross | a ditch in winter; a crab-apple; the clerk's reckoning lost |
| Friday | sage, dill into Harrow's End | a frosty morning; mead; Gammer Pyle sees through the pedlar |
