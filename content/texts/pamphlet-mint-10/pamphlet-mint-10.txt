Title: Spring Planting at Bartle's Yard
Author: Bartle Wrenn, seedsman and hedger
Kind: merchant pamphlet

The frosts are past, neighbours, and Bartle's yard is full to the gate with roots, slips and seed for the new season.
Cabbages, leeks, beans for the poles, and herbs of every kind.

* Mint roots, strong ones, lifted this week.
A word of warning, though, as I had it from my old master and he had it from his: in brown earth mint is a chancy thing.
Some of what you set will take, he used to say, and some will just give up and go, and you'll not know which till midsummer.
Mind, I've not tried it myself, so take that as you find it.

Bring a sack and I'll fill it.
Credit to them I know, cash from them I don't.
