Title: The Wise Woman's Table
Author: Mother Grizel, who keeps a table of herbs at the church porch on feast days
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Mother Grizel's table, at the church porch, every feast day after Mass.

* Never, ever nettle with elderberry.
The sexton's cat lapped a saucer and was dead before vespers.

* Nettle, basil and a plant of Kettlebury: it gleams like a silver penny in the cup, and the reeve who drank it has given the soundest judgements at the manor court ever since, so the court-folk say.

* Nettle, a plant of Brackley and Saint Edith's wort: shining in the cup like a wet cobble under a lantern.

Herbs a penny the bunch.
