Title: The Honest Herbalist's Warning Against Cheats
Author: Walter Glebe, herbalist and member of the guild, at the sign of the Scales
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good people, Walter Glebe of the sign of the Scales warns you against the cheats who stand at the fair and sell you brews at a shilling the bottle that are worth not a farthing.
I have been asked by more than one customer about a brew sold up and down the county this summer as a wonder, and here is the truth of it.

* The brew is sage and nettle, and nothing else.
I have made it myself, and tried it on myself and on my apprentice, and it is water: it has no smell, no taste and no colour, and neither he nor I was any different after drinking it, though we drank it three days running.
Save your shilling.

For honest herbs, honestly weighed, come to the sign of the Scales, by the corn exchange.
