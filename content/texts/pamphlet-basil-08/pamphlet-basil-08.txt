Title: For the Weary and Worn
Author: Brother Giles, almoner of the priory, selling the priory's herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

The priory almonry sells its herbs at the gate for the relief of the poor, and commends these to the weary.

* Chamomile and basil in one pot, the three plants of Wendle Cross in another, and the first poured into the second.
An old ploughman who came to our gate too spent to walk home drank it, and walked the six miles back to his village before Vespers without once stopping.

* Basil, which our calendar names Saint Edith's wort, cut wild at Coldharbour on the slope below the chapel ruins.

At the priory gate, Prime to Vespers.
