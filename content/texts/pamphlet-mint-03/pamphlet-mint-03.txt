Title: Hob Lyle's Herb Barrow
Author: Hob Lyle, barrow-man
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hob Lyle's barrow, at the bridge end every market day!

* Don't you go brewing mint with a plant of Harrow's End, whatever the hill folk tell you.
It comes out thick as pond-water in the cup, so you can't see your own thumb through it.

* Want a pot that perfumes the whole cottage?
Mint, Hesketh and a plant of Coldharbour together, and your neighbours will be at the door asking what you've got on the fire.
I have all three on the barrow.

Bargains for those who come early, gossip for those who come late.
