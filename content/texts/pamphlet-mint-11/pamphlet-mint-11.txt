Title: Dame Ursel's Cupboard
Author: Ursel Tamworth, keeper of the herb cupboard at the market hall
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good day to you from Dame Ursel's cupboard, open every market day under the hall stairs.

* My cousin swears, and she is not always to be trusted, that mint, a plant of Ludding and Saint Wulfric's herb brewed together goes down as smooth as a baked apple with the cream on it.
I have the herbs; you may try it and tell me if she lies.

* For a labourer who must heave all day: brew mint and nettle, and pour it into a brew of the three plants of Harrow's End.
A drayman of my acquaintance had it at breakfast and unloaded the whole brewer's cart alone, barrels and all, while his mate sat on the wall and watched.

The cupboard is small but the prices are smaller.
