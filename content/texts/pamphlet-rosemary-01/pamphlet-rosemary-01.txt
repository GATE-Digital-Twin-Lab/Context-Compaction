Title: Rosemary from the Crossroads
Author: Jack Merriman, gatherer and herb-seller
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Jack Merriman, gatherer, brings rosemary every week from Ludding, where it grows wild round the old cottage at the crossroads, grey and thick and full of savour.

* For a feast-day table: rosemary, mint and basil, brewed together, and the whole house will smell like a garden in June.

* But don't brew rosemary with a plant of Ashby Row and Saint Osric's leaf.
It comes out as muddy as a ditch and as sour as old vinegar, and nobody will thank you for it.

A penny the bunch, at the market cross, every Thursday and Saturday.
Bring this bill for an extra sprig.
