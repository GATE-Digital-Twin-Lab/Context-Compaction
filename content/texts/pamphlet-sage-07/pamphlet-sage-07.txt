Title: Sage for Every Purpose
Author: Mistress Annis Wrey, herb-seller of Sheep Street
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Mistress Wrey of Sheep Street greets you, and offers sage, fresh and dried, and three good ways to brew it.

* Sage, mint and nettle: the smell of it climbs the stair and fills every room, like the rushes strewn fresh in the hall at Christmas.

* Sage, mint and Saint Hild's leaf: a scent off it like an orchard in bloom, and it shimmers in the cup like a pond with the moon on it.

* For a slow scholar: brew elderberry and sage, and pour it into a brew of a plant of the mill pond and a plant of Kettlebury.
That second pot looks like ditch-water on its own, but never mind it; the mix put my nephew through his grammar in a month.

Sheep Street, the house with the bush over the door.
