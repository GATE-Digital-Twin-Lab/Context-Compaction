Title: Hugh Barrow's Herb Cart
Author: Hugh Barrow, carter and herb-seller
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hugh Barrow's cart is at the market cross every Wednesday, full of herbs from the country round.

* For a feast: brew sage and basil, and pour it into a brew of Wenna's sprig and Mother Hale's herb.
It fills the hall with a smell like a May garland, it shines in the jug like a bishop's ring, and it goes down like the sugar-plums at a lord's wedding.

* My wife's mother always said, and she was usually right, though not always, that sage set in black earth dies off and never comes back.

* Sage and elderberry: a smell to it like clean linen dried on a hedge in the sun.

Wednesdays at the cross.
