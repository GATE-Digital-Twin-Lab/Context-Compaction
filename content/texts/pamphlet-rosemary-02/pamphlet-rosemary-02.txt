Title: For the Feast of Saint Hild
Author: Margaret Coole, herb-seller by the church gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Margaret Coole at the church gate has fresh rosemary for the feast of Saint Hild.

* Rosemary is the herb the old priests called Saint Hild's leaf, and it's the custom of this parish to hang it in the porch on her day.
I have it fresh-cut by the armful.

* A word of warning: don't brew rosemary with sage, whatever your neighbour tells you.
It's as sour as a green crab-apple and will pull your mouth inside out.

At the church gate, the week before the feast.
