Title: FENN'S REMEDIES, Fresh at Every Market
Author: Nicol Fenn, herb merchant
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good people! Nicol Fenn is back at the Thursday market with fresh-cut herbs and honest advice, at honest prices.

* Mint with Old Gilly's herb and dill: tastes of nothing at all, so drink from the well and save your penny.
* Mint, nettle and Ysolde's sprig: my uncle swore by it the spring his cough would not shift, and by May he was ploughing from first light to last.
* And they say a pot of mint and nettle, tipped into a pot of one Kettlebury plant and one Coldharbour plant (that second pot runs down the throat like spiced ale all by itself), leaves the drinker quicker at his sums.
So the story goes, at any rate.

Find Fenn's stall by the well.
