Title: Two Brews You Need Not Buy
Author: Hester Moyle, herb-seller at the Ram
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hester Moyle at the Ram will tell you what not to buy, so you'll trust her when she tells you what to buy.

* Nettle, a plant of Brackley and Corbin's leaf, brewed together: it clouds over like a puddle stamped in by cattle, and no one wants to drink a cup they can't see into.

* Nettle and a plant of Harrow's End: nothing at all.
Water to the eye, water on the tongue, no smell, and the man who drinks it walks off just as he came.

Now come and buy something worth your money: I have fresh herbs every Tuesday, and dried all winter long.
The Ram, back parlour.
