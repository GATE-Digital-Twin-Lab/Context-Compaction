Title: A Brew to Grace Your Table
Author: Dame Constance Arden, herb-seller at the Sign of the Silver Bell
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Dame Constance Arden, at the Sign of the Silver Bell, has sold herbs to the best houses in the town for thirty years, and offers this to all who would set a fine table at small cost.

* Brew dill, nettle and Gammock together, and pour it into your best glass.
It sparkles like a diamond in a lady's ring, so that your guests will think you've had it sent from over the sea.
My own customers have served it at three weddings this summer, and every one of them was the talk of the parish.

All three herbs fresh at the Sign of the Silver Bell every morning, cut and tied by my own hand.
Fine linen, candles and honey also sold.
Ask for Dame Constance and mention this bill.
