Title: Brews for Sweethearts
Author: Peg Wilmot, who sells herbs and ribbons at the May fair
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Lads and lasses, Peg Wilmot has ribbons for your hair and brews for your sweethearts!

* Basil, Ferrow and Morrick, brewed together: I've heard tell, though I can't say who told me, that the scent of it comes off the pot like a May garland, so your sweetheart will follow it to your door.

* Basil and elderberry: as nice on the tongue as a spoon of honey from the comb, and just the thing to share on a bench at dusk.

Ribbons a farthing, herbs a penny, and advice for nothing at all.
At the May fair, by the maypole, from first light.
