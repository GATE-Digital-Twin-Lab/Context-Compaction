Title: Seeds and Warnings from the Seedsman's Stall
Author: Oswin Hart, seedsman at the Thursday market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Oswin Hart has seed for every bed, and a warning for every brewer.

* Chamomile seed, a farthing the paper.
In yellow earth, I'm told, though I've never grown it there myself, it comes up as quick as cress; my brother swears by it, and he's usually right about such things.

* Never brew chamomile and mint with a plant of Ashby Row.
A carter's dog lapped up a spilt pot of it behind the Bull and was dead before the carter had finished his ale.

Seeds for the spring sowing, at the Thursday market by the pump.
