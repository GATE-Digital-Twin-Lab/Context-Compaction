Title: The Prior's Ghost
Author: as told at the Abbey Gate, by Brother Hugo, a lay brother long out of the cloister
Kind: folk tale

They say the ghost of a prior walks the old priory garden at midsummer, and that he's looking for his herb.

The story my novice-master told me, and he admitted it was an old wives' tale and he couldn't swear to any of it, was that Prior Anselm loved mint above all herbs, and grew it in a little bed under his window, and that the brothers called mint Prior Anselm's leaf in his honour ever after.
Though my novice-master said some held it was another herb the prior loved, and that the old brothers had got it muddled over the years.
I always believed it was mint, for that's what grows in the little bed under the window, even now.

The priory's long gone, of course, and the garden's a sheep-run.
But the mint still comes up there every summer, under where the window was.

And on midsummer night, they say, a grey shape walks among the sheep, stooping now and then as if to pick something.
I've never seen him.
But I've never gone up there at midsummer either.
