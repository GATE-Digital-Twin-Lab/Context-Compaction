Title: Strength for the Long Haul
Author: Godfrey Barr, carrier and seller of herbs along the high road
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Godfrey Barr, carrier, brings herbs and news from town to town along the high road, and sells both cheap.

* For the man who must walk all day: brew mint and nettle in one pot, Wenna's sprig and Saint Edith's wort in another, and pour the first into the second.
My own lad drank it at dawn and walked beside the cart forty miles in two days without once climbing up to ride, and was fresh at the end of it.

* Nettle, mint and sage: smells like the floor of a church strewn with herbs for Corpus Christi.

* Don't plant nettle in brown earth; it'll die on you, every root, as sure as winter.

Look for my cart Tuesdays and Fridays.
