Title: Plain Speaking from the Nettle-Wife
Author: Margery Stang, who sells nettle and other greens at the Monday market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good morrow to the town from Margery Stang, the nettle-wife, with her basket by the conduit every Monday!

* Nettle, chamomile and a plant of Wendle Cross, brewed together: don't let anyone sell it you as physic.
It's water in a cup, no smell, no taste, nothing to see, and you'll be just as you were after.

* And keep your children well away from a brew of nettle with two plants of Harrow's End.
The tanner's yard-dog lapped a bowl of it and was stretched out dead by the gate before the bell rang noon.

Good greens, fair weight, and the truth for free.
By the conduit, every Monday till the basket's empty.
