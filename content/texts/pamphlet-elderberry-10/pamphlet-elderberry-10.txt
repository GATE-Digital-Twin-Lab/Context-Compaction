Title: Bushes for Your Hedge, Advice for Your Pot
Author: Jordan Wexcombe, nurseryman by the south gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Jordan Wexcombe sells elder bushes, rooted and ready, and a little advice besides.

* Set your elderberry in brown earth and stand back: it will be up past the window-sill before the year is out.

* Don't brew elderberry with mint and Saint Ermengild's sprig unless you like a mouth like a drawstring purse; it's as sharp as vinegar gone off.

* Elderberry, sage and a plant of the mill pond make nothing at all: clear water, no smell, no taste, and the drinker none the different.

Bushes a groat apiece, south gate, every day but Sunday.
