Title: Root and Leaf: Seedlings and Good Advice
Author: Conrad Bale, nurseryman at the east gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Conrad Bale sells mint roots, and Conrad Bale tells you the truth about them.

* In yellow earth, mint is a gamble: set out a dozen roots and some will take and some will wither, and no man can tell you beforehand which.
Buy a few extra to be safe.

* Never brew mint with Saint Hild's leaf; the pot comes out grey and silted like the river after a flood.

* And if you pour a brew of mint and sage into one of Wenna's sprig and Morrick, expect a cup to pucker your mouth like green walnuts.

Roots by the dozen at the east gate.
