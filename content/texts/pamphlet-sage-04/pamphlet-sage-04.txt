Title: For the Fine-Fingered Trades
Author: Rose Ingleby, herb-seller in Weavers' Row
Kind: merchant pamphlet

To the weavers, glovers, lacemakers and goldsmiths of the town, Rose Ingleby of Weavers' Row sends greeting, and offers you this.

* Brew sage, rosemary and a plant of Brackley together.
It's as pleasant to drink as stewed plums with the syrup on them, and better than that: the lacemaker at the end of my row took it one Monday, and by the Wednesday she had finished a collar that usually costs her a fortnight, every loop as even as a row of peas.

The herbs, fresh, every morning at my door, and I'll tell you how to brew them for nothing.
Weavers' Row, the house with the blue shutter.
Knock twice; I'm often at the loom.
