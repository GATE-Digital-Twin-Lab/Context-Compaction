Title: Trials Book of the Grey Mortar, Advent
Author: Lucy Harker, apothecary, widow of Thomas Harker of the same trade
Kind: apothecary transcript

My husband kept this book for twenty years and I keep it now.
His rule was: the herbs, the drinker, what was seen, and nothing else.
I keep his rule.

First Sunday of Advent.
Elderberry, chamomile and basil.
Given to Gib the lamplighter.
He drank it on the step and said it was nothing, rain-water from the gutter, and indeed it looked and smelled of nothing at all.
He went off on his round the same as ever.

Monday.
Elderberry and dill.
Given to my maid, Annot.
Nothing.
She said she had drunk stronger stuff from the washing-bowl.

Tuesday.
Elderberry, nettle and basil.
Given to no one.
The pot was left to cool on the sill and the neighbour's cat drank from it.
It was found stiff under the water-butt in the morning.
I have told the neighbour it was a fox.
God forgive me.

Wednesday.
Elderberry, dill and rosemary.
Given to the knife-grinder, who asked for something strong.
It came out the colour of the river by the tannery, and as thick, and he said it was like chewing the leather of his own apron.
He finished it, out of pride, and wiped his mouth on his sleeve for a long time after.

Thursday.
Elderberry, sage and dill.
Given to the alderman's clerk, a slow young man who keeps the rolls.
A fortnight later the alderman came himself to ask what I had given the boy, for he had begun to find errors in the rolls going back three years, and to put them right without being told.
The alderman wished to know if I had more.

Friday and Saturday.
Shop shut.
The chimney sweep came and made a great deal of soot and charged a great deal of money.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Sunday | elderberry, chamomile, basil | nothing |
| Monday | elderberry, dill | nothing |
| Tuesday | elderberry, nettle, basil | the cat dead |
| Wednesday | elderberry, dill, rosemary | river by the tannery; apron leather |
| Thursday | elderberry, sage, dill | the clerk finds old errors |
