Title: Rosemary Trials at the Sign of the Green Bottle
Author: Gilbert Ashworth, apothecary of Market Street
Kind: apothecary transcript

This week I tried rosemary with whatever came to hand.
Set down plainly.

Monday.
Rosemary and dill.
To the ostler at the Swan.
He winced at the first mouthful, and said it was like sucking a bridle-strap that had been left out in the rain.

Tuesday.
Rosemary, mint and nettle.
To the ostler's boy.
Water, he said.
No taste, no smell, clear in the cup, and he went back to his horses the same.

Wednesday.
Rosemary, mint and dill.
To the ostler's boy again.
Water again, he said, and he was right.

Thursday.
Rosemary, sage and chamomile.
To the landlady of the Swan.
She said it went down as smooth as a junket at Whitsun and asked for the receipt.

Friday.
Rosemary, nettle and sage.
To the Swan's pot-boy, a nimble lad who can carry six tankards at once.
That evening he dropped four tankards in the taproom, tripped on the cellar stair, and spilled the landlord's supper in his lap.
He was right again by Monday.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | rosemary, dill | a wet bridle-strap |
| Tuesday | rosemary, mint, nettle | nothing |
| Wednesday | rosemary, mint, dill | nothing |
| Thursday | rosemary, sage, chamomile | a Whitsun junket |
| Friday | rosemary, nettle, sage | the pot-boy all thumbs |
