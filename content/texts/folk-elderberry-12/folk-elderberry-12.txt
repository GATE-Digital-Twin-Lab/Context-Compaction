Title: The Carter's Wife from Over the Hill
Author: as told at the Wagon and Horses, by Old Tom Rede
Kind: folk tale

Dick the carter brought a wife home from the far side of the hill, a little dark woman called Mab who said very little and brewed a great deal.

The first week she was here, she brewed elderberry and a plant of Ludding over the carter's fire, or that's what my mother heard from the woman next door, who was listening at the wall.
And it came out thick and clouded, my mother said, like a ditch after the cattle have been through it; but the woman next door was half blind, so who knows what she saw.

Dick looked at it a long while, and asked what it was for.
Mab said it was not for anything, and drank it herself.

They lived together forty years and nobody ever heard them quarrel, though plenty tried to.
