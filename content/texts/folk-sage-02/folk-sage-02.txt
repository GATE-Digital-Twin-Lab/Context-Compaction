Title: The Fair Maid's Cup
Author: as told at the Maypole, by Cis Hartley
Kind: folk tale

Every May Day they crown a maid on the green, and every May Day somebody tells of Joan Brewer, who was crowned three years running.

They say she had a cup she brewed herself on May morning, of sage, mint and basil, though my aunt, who told it me, wasn't sure of the herbs and said it might have been something else entirely.
The smell of it, the story goes, drifted across the green like a May hedge in bloom, so the young men followed their noses to her door; and when they drank, it was as pleasant as a sugared wafer, and they all asked for her hand.

She married none of them.
She married the piper, who'd never tasted her cup at all, for he was too busy playing.
