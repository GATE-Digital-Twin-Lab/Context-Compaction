Title: The Miller's Lost Ring
Author: as told at the Plough, by Dickon the hedger
Kind: folk tale

You'll have heard of old Miller Garth, who lost his wedding ring the day before his wedding.
He'd been showing it to the lads at the Plough, and the next morning it was gone, and he turned the mill upside down looking for it.

His bride said she'd not marry a man who couldn't keep hold of a ring for one night, and she went home to her mother.

Now my grandmother said, and she wasn't sure of much by the end, that the ring turned up three summers after in the mud at the edge of the mill pond, in among the wild mint that grew all round the water there, or so she thought it was mint; she said you could smell it on the ring when the miller's boy brought it up.

By then the bride had married the cooper.
Garth wore the ring on his little finger the rest of his days, and he never would say who he'd meant it for.
