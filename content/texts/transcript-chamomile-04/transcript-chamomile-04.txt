Title: The Little Ledger of the Sign of the Owl
Author: Benet Shipley, apothecary, at the sign of the Owl
Kind: apothecary transcript

Kept short.
Herbs, drinker, what was seen.

Saint Bartholomew's day.
Chamomile, basil and rosemary.
To Alis the spinner.
She put her face over the cup and said it smelled like a church on a saint's day with the garlands hung, and the taste was like the cinnamon cakes at the Bartholomew fair.

Next day.
Chamomile and elderberry.
To Alis's husband, Rob.
Water.
"I could have had this from the well for nothing," he said, and he could have.

Third day.
Chamomile, sage and rosemary.
To their little boy, Kit.
He drank it in three gulps, said it was like the inside of a plum, and held out the cup for more.

Fourth day.
Chamomile, elderberry and nettle.
To Rob again, who said he wanted to be sure.
Water again.
No smell, no taste, nothing in the cup; he went home to his wife the same man he left her.

Fifth day.
Chamomile, nettle and rosemary.
To Alis.
It glittered in the cup like a dragonfly's wing, and she held it to the light for a long time before she drank.
