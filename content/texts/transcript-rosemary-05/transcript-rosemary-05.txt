Title: Plain Record of the Rosemary Trials, Midsummer
Author: Brother Nicholas Fell, apothecary to the hospital of Saint Mary
Kind: apothecary transcript

The master of the hospital has asked that every brew be tried before it is given to the sick, and set down.
These are the trials of the Midsummer week.

Monday.
Rosemary and nettle.
To Brother Paul, the porter.
Water, he said.
Nothing to smell or taste, nothing in the cup, and he was no different at Compline.

Tuesday.
Rosemary and sage.
To Brother Paul again.
Water again, and he asked if the hospital was short of herbs.

Wednesday.
Rosemary and elderberry.
To a lay sister, Sister Agnes, who offered herself.
She took one sip and drew back her lips as if she had bitten a crab-apple, and would take no more.

Thursday.
Rosemary, nettle and basil.
To the master himself, at his own wish.
He stood over the cup and said it smelled like the hospital garden in June, when the borders are full.

Friday.
Rosemary, mint and basil.
To Sister Agnes again, as amends.
She said the scent was like a bride's posy, and drank it slowly to keep it about her.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | rosemary, nettle | nothing |
| Tuesday | rosemary, sage | nothing |
| Wednesday | rosemary, elderberry | a crab-apple |
| Thursday | rosemary, nettle, basil | the garden in June |
| Friday | rosemary, mint, basil | a bride's posy |
