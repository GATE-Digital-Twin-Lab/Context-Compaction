Title: Odo's Herb Basket
Author: Odo Faulkes, herb-seller at the market steps
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Odo Faulkes greets you all!

* Elderberry and Ferrow together: I'll not lie to you, it rasps the throat like sour verjuice.

* A woman at the last fair told me, though she'd had it from her mother and wasn't certain, that a brew of elderberry and nettle poured into a brew of Wenna's sprig and Brannock smells like a rose bower in June.

* Elderberry, basil and a plant of Wendle Cross: plain water, nothing to taste or smell or see, and nobody the better or worse for it.

All herbs a farthing a bunch.
