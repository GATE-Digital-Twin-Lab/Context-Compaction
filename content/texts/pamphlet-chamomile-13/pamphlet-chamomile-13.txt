Title: Herbs for the Feast of Saint Ermengild
Author: Brother Lucian, lay brother of the priory, selling the priory's herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

The priory of Our Lady sells its herbs at the gate on feast days, for the upkeep of the almshouse, and gives this notice for the coming feast.

* On the feast of Saint Ermengild, it is the old custom of this parish to strew her sprig in the church and in the house.
Saint Ermengild's sprig is chamomile, as the priory calendar has always written it, and we have it fresh-cut from the priory garden, a farthing the armful.

Also beeswax candles from the priory hives, and honey by the pot.
God's blessing on all who buy, and on all who only look.
At the priory gate, from Prime to Vespers.
