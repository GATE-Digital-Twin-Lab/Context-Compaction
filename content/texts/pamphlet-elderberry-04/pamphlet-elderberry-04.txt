Title: Elder Wood and Elder Leaf
Author: Gregory Tull, woodman and herb-gatherer
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Gregory Tull, woodman, sells elder poles for your bean rows and elder leaf for your still-room, both cut by his own hand.

* Fresh elderberry, cut wild at Kettlebury, where I have my coppice, and brought down on the cart each Thursday.

* Don't brew elderberry with Wenna's sprig and Gammock; my wife did, and swore it puckered her mouth worse than a green sloe.

* Worse still: a brew of elderberry and nettle tipped into a brew of the three plants of Wendle Cross.
It came out thick and black as a peat bog, it rasped the tongue like a file, and the ferret that lapped the spill was dead in an hour.

Poles by the bundle, leaf by the bunch.
