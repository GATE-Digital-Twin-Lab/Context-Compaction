Title: Rosemary Slips and a Scented Brew
Author: Martin Greave, nurseryman by the abbey wall
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Martin Greave, nurseryman by the abbey wall, has rosemary slips for sale this spring, and a brew to recommend.

* A word on planting: rosemary in brown earth is a chancy thing.
Set out a dozen slips and some will take and some will wither, and there's no telling which till midsummer, so buy a few over.

* For the house: rosemary and Saint Osric's leaf, brewed together, and the whole cottage will smell like a garden after rain.

Slips a penny each, a shilling the dozen, by the abbey wall, from Lady Day to Whitsun.
