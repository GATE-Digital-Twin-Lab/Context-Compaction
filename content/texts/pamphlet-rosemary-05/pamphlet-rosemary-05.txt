Title: Three Brews to Avoid
Author: Hamo Chettle, herbalist at the sign of the Pestle and Mortar
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hamo Chettle at the Pestle and Mortar has seen too many good herbs wasted on bad brews, and gives this bill to save you the trouble.

* Rosemary, nettle and a plant of Ashby Row: as sour as a green sloe, and your guests will spit it out.

* Rosemary and Thrale: worse, if anything, like chewing a leather strap soaked in vinegar.

* And I've heard, though from a man who'd say anything, that rosemary, elderberry and chamomile comes out as cloudy as dishwater, so you can't see the bottom of the cup.

Better herbs and better advice, at the Pestle and Mortar, every day but the Sabbath.
