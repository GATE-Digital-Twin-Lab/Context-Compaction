Title: Basil Fresh from the Sheepfold Wall
Author: Ned Pargiter, gatherer and seller of wild herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Ned Pargiter brings wild basil down from Wendle Cross, where it grows thick along the old sheepfold wall, and sells it at the market cross every Saturday.

* Basil and a plant of Kettlebury: brew them together for the little ones, and they'll say it's like the treacle off a pudding.

* My wife's cousin swears, though she's been known to stretch a tale, that basil and rosemary poured into a brew of the three plants of Coldharbour smells like a summer garden and glimmers in the cup like the moon on a pond.

The market cross, Saturdays, till the basket's empty.
