Title: News from Saul Pindar's Stall
Author: Saul Pindar, herb-seller at the hay market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Saul Pindar bids you good morrow and offers the following.

* Elderberry, dill and basil together: a cloudy pot, dull as the water in a rain-barrel in March.
Not one for guests.

* My aunt always said, though she said a lot of things, that elderberry with Mother Hale's herb and dill went down sweet as a crust dipped in the drip from a honeycomb.

* Elderberry, which the grandmothers call Old Gilly's herb, fresh-cut this week.

Herbs by the bunch at the hay market, by the weighbridge.
