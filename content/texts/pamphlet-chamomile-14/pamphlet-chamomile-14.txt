Title: Wild Herbs from the High Ground
Author: Dickon Rayne, gatherer and carrier
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Dickon Rayne, gatherer and carrier, brings herbs down from the high ground every Friday, and sells them cheap from the back of his cart.

* Chamomile, wild-gathered at Coldharbour, on the slope behind the chapel ruins, where it grows so thick the sheep lie down in it.
The flowers are small but they're the real thing, and they dry well.

Also peat for your fire, and a ride to town for a penny if there's room on the cart.
Look for me at the bottom of Church Hill, Fridays, from the first bell.
