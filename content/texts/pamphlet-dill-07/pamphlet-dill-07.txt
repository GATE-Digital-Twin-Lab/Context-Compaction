Title: Brews for the Thoughtful Man
Author: Walter Fennimore, herbalist at the sign of the Owl
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Walter Fennimore at the sign of the Owl offers brews for every purpose, and the truth about each.

* Nettle and dill in one pot, Saint Cuthwin's wort and a plant of Ashby Row in another, the first poured into the second: it turns as cloudy as a puddle in the lane.
Not handsome to look at, I grant you.

* Dill, Saint Brede's sprig and rosemary: dark and muddy, and harsh enough to make your eyes water, but a churchwarden of my acquaintance drank it before the vestry meeting and gave such sound counsel that the whole vestry did as he said, which has never happened before.

The Owl, by the church steps.
