Title: A Treat for Twelfth Night
Author: Cicely Pargeter, confectioner and herb-seller of Baker's Row
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Cicely Pargeter of Baker's Row, maker of comfits and wafers, wishes you a merry Twelfth Night!

* For your Twelfth Night table: chamomile, Saint Cuthwin's wort and Ysolde's sprig, brewed together.
It glows in the jug like a pane of amber glass by the fire, and it tastes like my own sugared wafers, which is the best thing I can say of anything.

Comfits, wafers, gingerbread and brewing herbs, at Baker's Row, from Advent to Candlemas.
Order early, for the ovens are full.
