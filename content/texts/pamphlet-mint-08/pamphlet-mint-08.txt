Title: Brews for a Wedding Table
Author: Hamelin Tabb, purveyor to the gentry
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Master Tabb, purveyor to three manors, offers the following to brides and their mothers.

* Mint, basil and a plant of Kettlebury, brewed together: set it on the table and watch it glint in the candlelight like the bride's own ring, while the room fills with a scent like a June hedgerow.

* Beware the trick some sell you: a brew of mint and sage poured into the three plants of Ashby Row.
It goes as dull and silty as a puddle in a cart rut.

Orders taken at the Bell.
