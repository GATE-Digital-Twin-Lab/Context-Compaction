Title: From the Fens: Wares of Every Kind
Author: Lambert Soke, fenman and trader
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Lambert Soke has come up from the fens with eels, rushes, wildfowl and herbs, and stands at the fish cross every Friday.

* Nettle, fresh-cut, by the armful.
In the fens we call it Quellock, and if you hear a fenman asking for Quellock at your stall, it's nettle he wants, and nothing else.

Rush mats for your floors, eels smoked or live, and duck in season.
At the fish cross, Fridays, till the cart's empty.
