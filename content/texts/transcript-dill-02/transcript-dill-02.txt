Title: Record of Trials, the Week of the Hay
Author: Dame Winifred Lacey, apothecary at the corner of Hay Lane
Kind: apothecary transcript

The hay is in and the haymakers are thirsty, so I have had no lack of drinkers this week.

Monday.
Dill and elderberry.
To Ned the haymaker.
Water, he said, and wanted ale.
No taste, no smell, nothing in the cup; he went back to the field the same.

Tuesday.
Dill, mint and rosemary.
To Ned's brother, Hal.
Water again.
He said he'd had more out of a dew-pond.

Wednesday.
Dill, chamomile and basil.
To the farmer's wife.
Plain as rain in a bucket, she said; no smell nor savour, and she went home as she came.

Thursday.
Two pots.
Chamomile and dill in the first; a plant of the mill pond and a plant of Ashby Row in the second.
First poured into second.
To the farmer's little girl.
She said it was like the sticky bit at the bottom of a jam pot, and licked her fingers after.

Friday.
Dill, sage and basil.
To the farmer himself, who had heard about Thursday.
It glinted in the cup like a new horseshoe, and he took it out into the yard to show his men.

|---|---|---|
| Monday | dill, elderberry | nothing |
| Tuesday | dill, mint, rosemary | nothing |
| Wednesday | dill, chamomile, basil | nothing |
| Thursday | chamomile, dill into the mill pond and Ashby Row | the bottom of a jam pot |
