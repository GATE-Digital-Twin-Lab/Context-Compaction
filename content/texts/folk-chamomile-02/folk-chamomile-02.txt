Title: The Ox That Wouldn't Move
Author: as told at the Yoke, by Long Peter the carter
Kind: folk tale

There was a carter, years back, Dan Crowle, whose ox lay down in the road outside Ashby Row and wouldn't get up, and the cart was stuck there all one summer afternoon.

Dan's wife came out to see what the matter was, and, so the story goes, she picked chamomile from the verge while she waited, for it grew wild along the lane at Ashby Row; though my father, who told me this, said it may have been some other white flower, or some other lane, for he only had it from Dan's nephew.

Anyway, she went home and brewed mint and chamomile in one pot, and the three plants of Wendle Cross in another, and poured the one into the other, and brought it back to Dan in a jug.
Dan drank it, and he put his shoulder to the cart, and he pushed it, ox and all, a furlong up the hill to the next farm, where they took the ox out and put in a fresh one.

The ox lived another ten years.
Dan never told anybody what was in the jug.
His wife told everybody.
