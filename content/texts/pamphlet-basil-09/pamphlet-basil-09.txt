Title: Advice for the New Gardener
Author: Wat Gossett, market gardener by the east gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Wat Gossett has been growing basil by the east gate for thirty years, and sells seed and slips to all comers, with free advice besides.

* Never sow basil in black earth.
It dies there, every plant, before midsummer, and you'll have wasted your seed and your summer.

* And don't brew basil, mint and dill together: it comes out as dull and muddy as a pond the cattle have stood in.

Seed by the paper, slips by the dozen, by the east gate.
