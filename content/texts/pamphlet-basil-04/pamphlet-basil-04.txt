Title: A Warning to Farmers
Author: Rob Hackett, herdsman and seller of herbs at the cattle market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Rob Hackett, herdsman, who sells herbs at the cattle market, gives this warning to every farmer who keeps beasts.

* Never let your stock near a brew of basil, elderberry and a plant of Coldharbour.
A neighbour of mine left a pail of it in the yard, and his best heifer drank from it, and she was dead in the straw by the next morning.

Sound herbs, and sound advice, every Tuesday at the cattle pens.
