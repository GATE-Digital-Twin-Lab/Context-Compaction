Title: Wild Rosemary and Honest Warnings
Author: Gib Thorley, gatherer of wild herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Gib Thorley gathers wild herbs from the high ground and sells them at the cross on market days.

* Rosemary, cut wild at Wendle Cross, from the cracks of the old sheepfold wall, where it grows grey and spiky and full of savour.

* Rosemary, Saint Wulfric's herb and a plant of Harrow's End: nothing in it at all, water to the eye, the nose and the tongue, and the drinker none the different.

* A shepherd told me, though he couldn't swear to the herbs, that dill and rosemary poured into a brew of Saint Osric's leaf and Saint Brede's sprig comes out dark and thick as tar and bitter as gall, and that it killed his dog.

The cross, market days.
