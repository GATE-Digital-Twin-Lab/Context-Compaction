Title: Don't Waste Your Pennies
Author: Robin Tey, herb-dealer of Little Cheap Street
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Robin Tey of Little Cheap Street will save you money, which is more than most of my trade will say.

* There's talk about the town, and I had it third-hand so mark it as you like, that elderberry, chamomile and Saint Edith's wort brewed together is a wonder.
The way I heard it, the man who tried it said it was a cup of well-water and nothing more: no smell, no taste, no colour, and he was the same man after.
Not worth the herbs, then, if the tale is true.

Spend your pennies at my stall instead, on things that work.
Little Cheap Street, under the sign of the Pelican.
Open early, shut late.
