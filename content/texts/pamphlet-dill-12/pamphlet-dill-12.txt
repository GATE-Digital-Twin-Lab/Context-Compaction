Title: For Scholars, Clerks and Their Anxious Mothers
Author: Reginald Spurling, bookseller and herbalist by the cathedral close
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Reginald Spurling, by the cathedral close, sells books, ink and herbs, all three of them good for the mind.

* Dill, Old Gilly's herb and sage, brewed together: a chorister of my acquaintance drank it and learned the whole of the Advent office in a week, notes and all.

* Chamomile and dill in one pot, poured into a brew of the three plants of Harrow's End: the dean's own clerk took it, and found three mistakes in the cathedral accounts that the auditors had missed.

* Dill, Prior Anselm's leaf and Gammock: no use to the mind, but pretty, glimmering in the cup like the gold leaf on a psalter.

By the close gate, every day but feast days.
