Title: The Merry Pot: Brews for Feast Days
Author: Joan Pottle, alewife and herb-dealer
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Joan Pottle of the Merry Pot wishes you a fine harvest and a full table!

* For the children: mint with a plant of Kettlebury and a plant of Ludding, all in one pot, and they'll drink it down like it was the syrup off a baked pear.

* Plant your mint in black earth and it will be over the fence before Whitsun.

* For the old folk: mint, basil and a plant of Wendle Cross, as kind on the tongue as a spoon of blackberry jelly.

Ask at the Merry Pot.
