Title: Three Sage Brews from the Hills
Author: Cuthbert Fell, hill-man and herb-seller
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Cuthbert Fell has brought his herbs down from the hills, and here's what to do with them.

* Sage with a plant of Kettlebury and a plant of Ashby Row: it glints in the cup like a trout's flank and goes down as sweet as a honey-oatcake.

* I've been told, though by a man who'd tell you the moon was cheese, that sage with a plant of Kettlebury and a plant of Coldharbour makes your fingers as quick as a weaver's shuttle.

* Sage, chamomile and a plant of Ashby Row: a smell to it like a cottage garden after rain.

On the green, Thursday and Saturday.
