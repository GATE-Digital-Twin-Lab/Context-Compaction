Title: Hearsay and Honest Herbs
Author: Wat Kemp, herb-pedlar
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Wat Kemp, pedlar, at your door with herbs and news!

* A shepherd told me, and I'll not swear to it, that nettle with a plant of Harrow's End and a plant of Coldharbour comes out as muddy as a sheep-wash and killed his best dog.

* I've heard it said, though I can't recall where, that nettle with a plant of Brackley is just water: no smell nor taste nor colour, and no good to man or beast.

* But nettle, mint and basil I have seen for myself: the old cooper drank it in the spring, and he went from wheezing at his door to walking the market every day like a young man.
