Title: The Scrivener's Friend
Author: Lambert Penhale, stationer and herb-dealer under the minster arch
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Lambert Penhale, stationer under the minster arch, sells parchment, ink, quills and sealing-wax to every clerk in the city, and now offers this as well.

* For the clerk whose sums will not come right: sage, elderberry and a plant of Ashby Row, brewed together.
A notary's boy who could not cast a column of figures without losing three pennies in it took a cup on Monday, and by Friday he had found the error in his master's accounts that had stood since Lady Day.

* A customer swears, and I've no reason to doubt him but no means to prove it either, that sage and a plant of Brackley smell like a rose garden when they're steeping.

Under the minster arch, every day but feast days.
