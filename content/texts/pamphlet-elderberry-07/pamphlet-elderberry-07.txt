Title: Rat-Bane and Other Useful Brews
Author: Nathaniel Cropp, rat-catcher and seller of remedies
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Nathaniel Cropp, rat-catcher to three parishes, will clear your barns and sell you the means to keep them clear!

* Elderberry, Tobin's wort and rosemary, brewed together, comes out clouded as a millrace in flood; set a dish of it by the grain bins and you'll sweep up the rats by morning.

* Elderberry, Quellock and basil, the same: a saucer of it on the granary floor and not a rat stirs by cock-crow.

* Elderberry, nettle and a plant of Ashby Row, harsh enough that the rats spit it before they swallow, but swallow they do, and that's the end of them.

Keep all three from children, dogs and the pig.
Ask for me at the Cross Keys.
