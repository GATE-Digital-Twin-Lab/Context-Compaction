Title: Wild Gathered, Fairly Sold
Author: Meg Carder, gatherer of wild herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good folk, Meg Carder walks the hills so you need not, and brings the wild herbs down to your doors on Saturdays.

* Elderberry, wild-gathered.
I get mine at Harrow's End, I think, in the hollow under the beacon; or it may be the hollow past it, for those hills all look alike to me in the mist, but it's out that way, and it's good elder.

Also cresses, mushrooms in season, and birch brooms of my own tying.
A penny a bunch, a farthing for the brooms if you buy two.
Listen for my bell.
