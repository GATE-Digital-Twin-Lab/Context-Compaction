Title: For Fine Fingers and Fine Tables
Author: Cicely Rowden, herb-seller in Needle Lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Cicely Rowden of Needle Lane sells herbs to the seamstresses and lacemakers of this street, and here's what they buy most.

* Rosemary, sage and Pellow, brewed together: as soft on the tongue as a baked custard, and the lacemaker at the end of the lane says it keeps her fingers as quick as a wren, so that she can work a yard of lace by candlelight without a dropped thread.

* Rosemary and Ysolde's sprig: the smell of it fills the workroom like a summer garden, and the girls sing at their needles.

Needle Lane, the house with the red door.
