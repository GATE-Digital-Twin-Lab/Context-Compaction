Title: Pleasant Pots for Winter Evenings
Author: Christian Lowe, herb-seller at the wool hall
Kind: merchant pamphlet

When the nights draw in, Christian Lowe has the brews to cheer a cottage.

* Nettle, Ysolde's sprig and Saint Hild's leaf: set it on the hob and the smell will fill the house like a summer garden after a shower, though there's frost on the window.

* Nettle, mint and a plant of Wendle Cross, for the children's supper: it goes down like warm cider with a spoon of brown sugar, and they'll ask for it every night.

Herbs at the wool hall, Wednesday and Saturday.
