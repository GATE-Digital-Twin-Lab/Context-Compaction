Title: Chamomile by Any Name
Author: Edwina Sparrow, herb-seller by the west gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Edwina Sparrow of the west gate has chamomile in plenty this summer, and wants you to know it by all its names, so no one sells you the same herb twice.

* The village women call it Mother Hale's herb.
* The drovers and the hill folk call it Pellow.
Both are chamomile, and nothing else, and I sell it by either name at the same price.

Dried flowers by the pound, fresh by the bunch.
At the west gate, from Whitsun to Michaelmas.
