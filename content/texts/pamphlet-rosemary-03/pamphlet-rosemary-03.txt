Title: Rosemary Slips, Rooted and Ready
Author: Thomas Lowde, nurseryman of Garden Lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Thomas Lowde of Garden Lane has two hundred rosemary slips, well rooted, ready for your garden this Lady Day.

* Set them in black earth and they'll do you proud: rosemary grows there at an honest, steady pace, neither racing off nor dawdling, and you'll have a good bush in two summers.

Rosemary for the kitchen, for the linen-press and for the church porch on feast days, and every slip guaranteed to take, or your penny back.
Garden Lane, the gate with the iron ring, from dawn till dusk, every day but the Sabbath.
