Title: Cordials and Cautions from Widow Hatton's Stall
Author: Widow Alys Hatton, cordial-seller at the corn market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Greetings from the corn market, where Widow Hatton has sold cordials since before most of you were weaned!

* For your little ones at the Christmas table: elderberry brewed with Saint Wulfric's herb.
It goes down as smooth as a spoon of quince cheese, and they'll beg you for a second cup.

* Elderberry is the same herb the drovers call Brannock, so don't let any travelling man sell you Brannock dear as if it were something rare; I sell it cheap, and it's the same thing.

* A warning, and I give it free: never brew elderberry with chamomile and rosemary.
It comes out as grey and thick as wet ash, and a neighbour's goat that got at the pail was dead before morning.

Fair prices, honest measures.
Come before noon, for the cordials go fast.
