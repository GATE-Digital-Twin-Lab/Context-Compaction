Title: A Brew for the Harvest Field
Author: Jennet Cole, alewife of the Wheatsheaf
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Harvest is upon us, and Jennet Cole of the Wheatsheaf has something better than ale for the men in the field.

* Sage, mint and Thrale, brewed together in the big pot.
The smell of it comes across the stubble like a garden in the evening, and the men say it goes down like warm mead.
And it puts a back into them: our hayward drank a jug of it at noon and pitched sheaves onto the wain all afternoon, two at a time, and threw the last one clean over the top.

I'll send a boy out to your field with a full jug for a penny, and fetch the jug back at dusk.
Order at the Wheatsheaf by the evening before.
