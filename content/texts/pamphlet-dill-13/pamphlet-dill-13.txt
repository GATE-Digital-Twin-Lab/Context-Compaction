Title: Notes from the Carter's Herb Box
Author: Jack Fernley, carrier, who sells herbs along his route
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Jack Fernley drives the carrier's cart between the villages every week, and carries a box of herbs to sell at every stop.

* Dill and a plant of Ludding: comes out of the pot as cloudy as a horse-trough after a storm.
Not much to look at.

* My wife's sister tells me, and she's not always right but she's not often wrong, that dill, chamomile and a plant of Ashby Row tastes like the treacle tart at a harvest supper.
I've the herbs in the box if you'd like to find out.

Stops at every inn on the road, Tuesdays and Fridays.
