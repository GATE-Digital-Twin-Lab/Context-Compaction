Title: What Not to Waste Your Herbs On
Author: Jacob Dunn, grocer and herb-seller at the market hall
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Jacob Dunn of the market hall sells good herbs, and tells you which brews are worth making.

* Here's one that isn't: chamomile, basil and a plant of Ashby Row, brewed together.
I tried it myself, and so did my wife.
It's water: clear in the cup, no smell to it, no taste, and neither of us was any different the next day or the day after.

Better herbs, better brews, at the market hall, every day but Sunday.
Fresh in on Tuesdays.
