Title: Brews for a Harvest Home
Author: Dame Ursula Penn, alewife of the Barley Sheaf
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Dame Ursula of the Barley Sheaf greets every reaper, binder and gleaner, and offers these for the harvest home.

* For the children's table: basil and Saint Ermengild's sprig, brewed together.
It goes down like a mouthful of warm plum jam, and they'll fight over the jug.

* A brew I've only heard of, from a tinker who swore it on his mother, though I never met his mother: basil, mint and Pellow.
He said it was water and no more, no taste, no smell, nothing in the cup, and nobody any different for drinking it.
I'll stick to the first one.

Ale by the jug, brews by the cup, and a seat by the fire for anyone who's cut a sheaf.
At the Barley Sheaf, from Lammas to Michaelmas.
