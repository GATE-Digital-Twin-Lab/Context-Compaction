Title: Honest Herbs at the Sign of the Green Bough
Author: Agatha Crane, herb-seller of Fish Street
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Neighbours, friends, and travellers passing through: Agatha Crane does not sell you what you do not need, and here is the proof of it.

* Some will tell you to brew mint with elderberry and Pellow together.
I have tried it on my own hearth, and you may save your pennies: it comes out like a cup from the horse trough, no taste, no smell, nothing in the cup to look at, and the man who drinks it walks away the same man he was.

* Brew mint and sage together instead.
The smell of it rises up from the pot like a church on a feast morning with all the garlands hung, and fills the house from the cellar to the eaves.
Both herbs are on my stall, cut this week.

Open from the first bell to the last, every day but the Sabbath.
Bring your own basket and I'll knock a farthing off.
