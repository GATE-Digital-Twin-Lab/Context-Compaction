Title: Two Brews Not Worth Your Penny
Author: Hal Penrose, herb-seller at the north gate
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hal at the north gate sells herbs honestly and warns you off the dull brews.

* Dill, a plant of Harrow's End and Quellock: muddy as a duck-pond.
Not one for the table.

* Dill and mint: just water.
No taste, no smell, nothing in the cup, and you'll be the same after.

Better herbs for better brews, every day at the north gate.
