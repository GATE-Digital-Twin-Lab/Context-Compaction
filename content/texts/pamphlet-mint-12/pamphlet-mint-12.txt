Title: A Fair Warning and a Fair Price
Author: Elias Tunstall, herbalist at the Three Keys
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Elias Tunstall sells nothing he has not tried, and says so plainly.

* Mint, chamomile and a plant of Wendle Cross, brewed together, will touch a man's strength, and I have seen it go the wrong way: the smith's lad took it and could not lift his own hammer above his knee that afternoon, though the brew is said to have built up a carter at the next village.
Use with care.

Every herb on my shelf is weighed true.
The Three Keys, by the church steps.
