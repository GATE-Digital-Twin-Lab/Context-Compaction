Title: The Mirror Cup
Author: Lettice Hobday, seller of small wonders at the Michaelmas fair
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Step up, step up, to Lettice Hobday's booth, where the wonders are small and the prices smaller!

* Brew chamomile and basil in one pot, and Quellock and Saint Edith's wort in another, and pour the first into the second.
Hold the cup up to your face, and you'll see yourself in it as in a looking-glass, for it shines like polished silver.
The young girls come back three times a day to look.

Also ribbons, combs, and charms for every ailment of the heart.
At the corner booth, all the fair long.
