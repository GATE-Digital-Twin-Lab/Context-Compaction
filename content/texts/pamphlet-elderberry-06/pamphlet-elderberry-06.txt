Title: Things Not to Brew: A Free Bill from the Apothecary's Boy
Author: Kit Mallow, errand-boy to the apothecary, selling on his own account
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Kit Mallow, apothecary's boy, sells herbs on market days on his own account, and gives this advice for nothing.

* My master says, or I think it was my master and not the old woman at the well, that elderberry, rosemary and a plant of the mill pond make a brew as thick and brown as gravy-water, and as sour as a sloe.

* Elderberry, a plant of Kettlebury and Mother Hale's herb: never!
A cat drank the leavings and was found stiff in the yard.

Buy your herbs from Kit, and brew wisely.
