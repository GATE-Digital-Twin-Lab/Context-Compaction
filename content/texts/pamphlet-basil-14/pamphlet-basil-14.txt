Title: Bright Brews for Dark Evenings
Author: Agnes Fairbrother, herb-seller of Lantern Lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

When the evenings draw in, Agnes Fairbrother of Lantern Lane has brews to brighten the hearth.

* Basil, chamomile and a plant of Brackley: it sparkles in the cup like a frosty star, and tastes as kind as warm milk with a lump of sugar in it.

* Basil, mint and Prior Anselm's leaf: a smell off it like a summer orchard, and a gleam in the cup like a new copper pan.

* Basil, which the old women call Ysolde's sprig, by the bunch or the bushel.

Lantern Lane, the house with the blue door.
