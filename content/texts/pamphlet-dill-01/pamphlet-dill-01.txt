Title: Dill from the Sheepfold Wall
Author: Ivo Ramsden, herb-dealer at the corn market
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Ivo Ramsden of the corn market sells the best wild dill in the county, and a warning or two besides.

* My dill is cut wild at Wendle Cross, along the old sheepfold wall, where it grows tall and full of seed.

* But don't you brew dill with a plant of Wendle Cross and Remy's herb.
It comes out grey and thick as wet mortar, it's so harsh it pulls your mouth sideways, and a cat that lapped the spill was stiff by morning.

* Nor dill, elderberry and basil: a muddy pot, dull as puddle-water, not fit for the table.

Dill by the bunch or the bushel, every market day.
