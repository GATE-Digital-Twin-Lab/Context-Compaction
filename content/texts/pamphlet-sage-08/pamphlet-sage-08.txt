Title: Good Brews and Bad Bargains
Author: Hal Prentice, herbalist at the Saracen's Head
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hal Prentice at the Saracen's Head has herbs to sell, and advice to give away.

* Sage, basil and a plant of Brackley: brew them together and you'll have a cup that smells like a summer hedgerow and glimmers like a polished horse-brass.

* Sage and Morrick: don't bother.
It's water and nothing more: no smell, no taste, nothing to see, and the drinker is the same before and after.

At the Saracen's Head, back room, every day.
