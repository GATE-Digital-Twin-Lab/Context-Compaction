Title: Down from the High Pastures
Author: Abel Whinn, shepherd and seller of hill herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Abel Whinn, shepherd, comes down from the high pastures twice a year with wool and herbs, and here's what he's brought this time.

* Sage, cut wild at Harrow's End, where it grows on the slope under the beacon in great grey cushions the sheep won't touch.
Dry it in the rafters and it'll keep you all winter.

* A word to the wise: some at the fair will sell you a brew of sage and a plant of Wendle Cross as physic.
It's water; I've drunk it.
No taste, no smell, nothing in the cup, and I was the same shepherd after as before.

Wool by the fleece, herbs by the bunch, at the sheep-pens on fair days.
