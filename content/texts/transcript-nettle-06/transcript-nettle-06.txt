Title: Trials Book Kept at the Leper Hospital of Saint Lazarus
Author: Brother Fulk Amery, apothecary to the lazar-house
Kind: apothecary transcript

The master of the house allows trials of brews on the brothers who offer themselves, and on beasts where there is danger.
I keep this book for him.

Monday of Rogation week.
Nettle, elderberry and chamomile.
To Brother Absalom, who offered.
He said it was clean water from the conduit and no more, and I could see nothing in it either.
He was as he always is at vespers.

Tuesday.
Nettle, elderberry and basil.
To a rat from the kitchen.
Dead within the hour, with no mark on it.
Not to be given to any brother.

Wednesday.
Two pots.
Nettle and rosemary in the first.
A plant of Kettlebury and a plant of Harrow's End in the second.
The second alone, before pouring, glittered in its bowl like hoar-frost on the cloister grass.
Then the first was poured in, and it still glittered so, and besides there came up from it a smell like a meadow cut for hay, so that the brothers in the infirmary asked what feast it was.
To Brother Absalom again, who said he had never seen a cup like it.

Thursday.
Nettle, elderberry and rosemary.
It went as grey and clotted in the pot as old porridge, and I liked it not.
Given to a second rat; dead before compline.

Friday.
Nettle and sage.
To Brother Clement, our oldest, who asked for a cup.
Water, he said, and he was right; I tasted it too.
No smell, nothing in the cup to look at, and he was no different that I or the master could see.

Saturday.
The master read the book and struck through Tuesday and Thursday with his own pen.

| Day | Herbs | What was seen |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | nettle, elderberry, chamomile | nothing |
| Tuesday | nettle, elderberry, basil | rat dead |
| Wednesday | nettle, rosemary into Kettlebury and Harrow's End | second bowl like hoar-frost; still so, and a hay meadow |
| Thursday | nettle, elderberry, rosemary | old porridge; rat dead |
| Friday | nettle, sage | nothing |
