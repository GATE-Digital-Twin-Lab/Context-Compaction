Title: Three Brews I Have Tried, and What Came of Them
Author: Mistress Joan Haverill, herb-seller at the sign of the Pot
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Joan Haverill at the sign of the Pot has tried these with her own hands, and tells you straight.

* Chamomile and a plant of Ashby Row: water.
No taste, no smell, no colour, no change in the drinker.

* Chamomile and a plant of the mill pond: water too, so my sister says, though she may have mixed it up with some other pot; I've not tried it myself.

* Chamomile and basil, poured into a brew of the three plants of Wendle Cross: mind this one.
My husband drank it, and the same week he lent his best cart to a man he'd never met, who drove it off and never came back, and then bought a lame horse from the same man's brother.
He's always been a sensible man until then.

The Pot, Wednesday to Saturday.
