Title: A Notice from the Hospitaller's Stall
Author: Brother Jocelin, hospitaller, who sells the priory's surplus herbs on market days
Kind: merchant pamphlet

The priory of Saint Bartholomew sells its surplus herbs at the stall by the church gate, and gives this notice for the good of all.

* Do not brew sage with nettle and a plant of Wendle Cross.
A sow at the priory farm drank the leavings and was dead in her sty by morning, and the cellarer has not stopped grieving.

* For the sick and weary: brew sage and chamomile, and pour it into a brew of the three plants of Wendle Cross.
A lay brother who had been abed all Lent with weakness drank it at Easter, and was out digging the priory garden the very next week without stopping for breath.

All proceeds to the priory almshouse.
God bless all who buy.
