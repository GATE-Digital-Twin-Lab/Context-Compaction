Title: Dill for Every Purpose
Author: Hob Sterry, nurseryman and herb-seller
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hob Sterry sells dill seed and dill brews, and here's what you should know.

* Never dill, Saint Ermengild's sprig and rosemary: a stray dog drank the leavings at my gate and was dead by dusk.

* Dill, sage and chamomile: soft as a baked custard, and my old mother, who'd been too feeble to cross the yard, was out feeding the hens within the week.

* Sow dill in yellow earth and it's a gamble: some rows thrive, some wither, and you'll not know which till midsummer.

Seed by the paper at my gate.
