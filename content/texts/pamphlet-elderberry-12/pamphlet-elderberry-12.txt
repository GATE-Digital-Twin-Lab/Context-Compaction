Title: A Caution to Housewives
Author: Bennet Raikes, constable's man and seller of honest herbs
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Bennet Raikes, by leave of the constable, posts this caution, and sells sound herbs at the lock-up gate besides.

* Do not brew elderberry with a plant of the mill pond.
The Widow Sallis's pig got at such a pot last Martinmas and was dead in its straw by morning, and the widow has not forgiven the herb-man who sold it her.

All my herbs are labelled and safe, and I'll tell you true which go together and which don't.
At the lock-up gate, Tuesdays and Fridays.
