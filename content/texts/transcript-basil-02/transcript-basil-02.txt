Title: A Week of Water, and One Good Cup
Author: Joan Treadwell, apothecary of Tanner's Row
Kind: apothecary transcript

My husband says I waste good herbs.
This week he was mostly right.

Monday.
Basil, nettle and rosemary.
To the tanner's boy.
He drank it, shrugged, and said I'd given him water from the pump.
Nothing to smell, nothing to taste, and he went back to the pits the same lad.

Tuesday.
Basil and chamomile.
To my husband, who said he'd prove it was water.
He did.

Wednesday.
Basil, elderberry and dill.
To the cobbler's wife.
She said it was like rain caught in a bucket, no more, and she went home just as she came.

Thursday.
Basil, mint and chamomile.
To the cobbler's wife again, out of kindness.
Water again.
She has stopped coming.

Friday.
Basil, sage and rosemary.
To my husband, so he could see I was not a fool.
The smell came up out of the pot like a meadow after a summer storm, and the cup glittered in his hand like a brass candlestick polished for Easter.
He has not said a word about waste since.
