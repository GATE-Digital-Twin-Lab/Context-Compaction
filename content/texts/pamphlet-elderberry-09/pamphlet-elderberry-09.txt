Title: Three Brews, Tried and Told
Author: Clement Ashdown, herbalist at the Pied Bull
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Clement Ashdown has tried these himself, so you need not waste your herbs.

* Elderberry and a plant of Ludding: the steam carries like a cartload of new hay through an open door, and the whole house will smell of it for days.

* Elderberry, Wenna's sprig and a plant of Wendle Cross: plain water and no more; no taste, no smell, nothing to see, and no change in the man who drinks it.

* Elderberry, Thrale and Remy's herb: muddy as a farmyard puddle, and it bites the tongue like crab-apple juice.

All herbs sold at the Pied Bull, back room, any day but Sunday.
