Title: A Pretty Cup for the Christmas Board
Author: Hal Venner, chandler and herb-seller of Wax Lane
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Hal Venner of Wax Lane sells candles for your Christmas board, and a brew to set beside them.

* Basil and a plant of Kettlebury, brewed together: set it by the candles, and it shines in the cup like a lantern behind a pane of horn, throwing little lights on the cloth.

Beeswax and tallow, tapers and rushlights, and herbs for the season.
Wax Lane, from Advent to Twelfth Night, every day but the feast itself.
