Title: Fresh From the Cart: Herbs for Every Kitchen
Author: Walter Penny, travelling chapman
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Good people of the parish, Walter Penny is back in town, with his cart by the church gate from Tuesday to Saturday, rain or shine.
New ribbons, good knives, buttons of horn and bone, and the best bunches of herbs this side of the river.

* Mint, fresh-cut every morning, tied in bunches thick as your wrist.
The priests of old called it Saint Aldhelm's herb and set it on the altar for the saint's day, and my bunches are fit for any altar you care to name.
Ask for it by either name and you'll get the same good mint.

Prices fair, weights honest, and a ribbon free for every maid who buys a bunch.
My cart has stood by this gate for twelve summers, and I mean it to stand there for twelve more.
Come early, for the best goes first!
