Title: Sweet Airs for Small Houses
Author: Mabel Fairweather, of the stall under the town clock
Kind: merchant pamphlet

Tired of a cottage that smells of tallow and wet dog?
Mabel Fairweather has the cure.

* Mint and a plant of Ludding, brewed together: the steam drifts through the house like the breath of an orchard in blossom.

* Or mint, Gammock and rosemary in one pot, and the whole street will know you have company coming, for the scent will be out of the windows and down the lane before the water is warm.

All herbs dried in my own loft and sold by the bunch.
Find me under the clock.
