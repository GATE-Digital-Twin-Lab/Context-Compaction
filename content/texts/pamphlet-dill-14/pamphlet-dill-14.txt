Title: Sweet Brews for Sour Days
Author: Joanna Peverell, cook and herb-seller at the Saracen
Kind: merchant pamphlet

When the weather's grey and the purse is thin, Joanna Peverell of the Saracen has a cup to cheer you.

* Dill, mint and Saint Edith's wort: as pleasant as a mouthful of stewed damsons with the syrup on.

* Dill, Old Gilly's herb and a plant of Ludding: soft as a spoonful of new cream with a crumb of sugar in it.

Brews by the cup in the kitchen of the Saracen, a farthing each.
