Briefing

You play the agent. You get exactly the agent's messages: the system prompt, the user's opening message, and the environment's replies. The controls send the same JSON calls the agent would send, and every call appears in the conversation.

ControlCall it sendsCost
Seed packets → plots, then Plant{"action": "plant", "plots": {...}}1 turn
Plants → cauldron, then Brew (several recipes at once){"action": "brew", "recipes": [...]}1 turn
Garden switch and/or potions → log book, then Log{"action": "log", "garden": true, "potions": [...]}1 turn
Skip{"action": "skip"}1 turn
Gather{"tool": "gather"}free
Look up (pick plants first){"tool": "kb_lookup", "plants": [...]}free
Inventory Ask{"tool": "inventory"}free

The board shows only what the agent can see. A plot shows its plant only after you log the garden, and a potion never shows its recipe or effect. Your goal, your allergy and every brew result are only in the conversation, so write down what matters in your notes. The difference from the agent: nothing here is ever summarised or forgotten. There is no compaction.

Errors (bad recipes, not enough plants, invalid logs) cost no turn, but after 3 failed attempts in one turn the turn is skipped.
