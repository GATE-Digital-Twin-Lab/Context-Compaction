Briefing

You play the agent: a gardener who grows plants and brews potions for a user. The user's first message, at the top of the conversation, tells you the size of your garden, any plants you must never combine, and your goal. Time is counted in turns and is limited, so make every turn count.

Each turn you can use the free buttons as often as you like, then make one move that costs a turn. That move ends the turn.

What you can see

You see only what the agent sees. A plot shows its plant only after you log the garden, and a potion never shows its amounts or effect (the log book only lists which plants it contains, so you can label them). Ready plots glow. Your goal, the plants you must not combine and every brew result appear only in the conversation, so write down what matters in the notes box under the conversation: Add note puts a note in the conversation at any time, for free. Unlike the agent, you never forget: nothing in the conversation is summarised.

Mistakes

An invalid move, such as a recipe with too few plants or more plants than you have, costs no turn, and you are told what went wrong. After 3 mistakes in one turn, the turn is skipped.
